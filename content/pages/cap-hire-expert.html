Hire an Expert is a marketplace for food know-how. Any T57 member can post a problem, compare verified experts by rating, reviews and price, and pay only when the job is done.

The problem

Advice comes from people with something to sell

A new farmer who finds pests or dying plants rarely gets help from a ministry. They go to the input shop, where advice comes from a salesperson, not an agronomist, and often means more chemical than the crop needs: money wasted and residues in food. Traders and processors face the same gap. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

No trusted expert nearby

Qualified agronomists, vets and food scientists are scarce where food is grown.

Advice tied to a sale

The person giving advice is often the person selling the product.

Hard to hire abroad

An importer who needs goods inspected in another country has no trusted way to find someone qualified.

Use case · Illustrative example

A new farmer in Madinah saves a greenhouse crop

A new farmer near Madinah sees leaf spots spreading across his tomato greenhouse and posts photos at night. By morning, three verified agronomists, in Egypt, India and Sudan, have sent offers. He picks a plant pathologist rated 4.9 who charges $15. On a 20-minute video call she identifies early blight, prescribes an approved treatment at the right dose and sets the last spray date before harvest. He pays from escrow and leaves a review.

FAQ

Common questions

How are experts checked?

Identity, qualifications and references are verified before an expert can take jobs.

What if the advice is wrong or the job is not done?

You can raise a dispute before payment is released. Upheld complaints count against the expert, and repeated ones lead to removal.

Do experts recommend chemicals?

Experts recommend only products approved in the client's country and record the dose they advise.

Can my company offer services?

Yes. Firms join as service providers; individuals join as experts.

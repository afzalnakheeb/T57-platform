The challenge

A single food trade still needs eight separate services

The world spends about $2.22 trillion a year importing food (FAO, 2025 forecast). Yet finding a buyer, checking them, agreeing terms, arranging finance, insurance, inspection, shipping and customs each come with their own paperwork, delays and risk. Banks rejected 41% of SME trade finance applications in 2025, part of a $2.5 trillion global gap (ADB, 2026).

Trust

Buyers and sellers cannot easily tell who is real, who owns the goods and who is only passing them on.

Fragmentation

Quotes, contracts, inspection reports, finance and shipping documents live in different inboxes and systems, so details get lost or changed.

Access to finance

Lenders cannot see a clean, verified history of a trade, so good businesses are turned away or charged more.

    FAQ

    Questions we hear most

    What is T57?

    T57 is AI-native digital trade infrastructure for the food industry. It brings verification, quotes, contracts, finance access, insurance, inspection and logistics onto one trade record for verified members.

    Who can join T57?

    Any business in the food chain can apply, including farmers, fishers, processors, manufacturers, brands, traders, importers, wholesalers, supermarkets and HoReCa buyers. Every member is verified before trading.

    Does T57 lend money or hold my funds?

    No. T57 does not lend, hold client funds, own goods or provide transport. Finance, escrow, insurance and logistics are provided by licensed partners through the platform.

    Which countries does T57 cover?

    T57's founding markets are Saudi Arabia, the UAE, India, Malaysia and Türkiye. Businesses in other countries can apply for membership online.

    How does T57 use AI?

    AI helps with matching, risk checks, document preparation and forecasting. People make the final decisions, and any adverse decision is reviewed by a person.

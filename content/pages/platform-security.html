T57's security architecture exists to protect the trade data, identities and documents that members trust to the platform, with layered controls, data residency by region and a full audit trail.

The problem

Food trade runs on sensitive information

Food trade runs on sensitive information: prices, buyers, bank details, identity documents and contracts. A single breach can expose a business to fraud or competitors, and many countries now require certain data to stay within their borders. Members and regulated partners need to know exactly how their data is protected and who can see it. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Sensitive data at risk

Trade, identity and payment details are valuable targets for fraud.

Data residency rules

Many founding markets require local storage or control of certain data.

Unclear access

Members cannot always see who accessed their information, and when.

How T57 protects your data

Verified identity → Least-privilege access → Protected data → Monitored and recorded

  1. 01

    Verified identity

    Every member is verified before trading, and every user signs in with strong, multi-factor authentication.

  2. 02

    Least-privilege access

    Role-based permissions mean each person and partner sees only what their role and trade require.

  3. 03

    Protected data

    Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and hosted on Microsoft Azure in multiple regions built for data residency.

  4. 04

    Monitored and recorded

    Activity is logged, monitored for unusual behavior and recorded in an audit trail, with key records anchored on a ledger.

Components

9 parts, one platform

Identity verification

KYC and KYB checks through licensed partners. For members, this means every counterparty is real.

Multi-factor authentication

Strong sign-in for every user. For members, this stops stolen passwords being enough.

Role-based access control

Permissions by role, company and trade. For businesses, this keeps staff and partner access tight.

Encryption

Data encrypted in transit and at rest. For members, this protects information if systems are compromised.

Regional hosting and data residency

Multi-region Azure hosting, with data kept in-region where rules require it. For members and regulators, this supports local data laws.

Consent management

Members control how their data is shared and used. For farmers and businesses, this means data used only with permission.

Audit trail

A record of who did what, and when. For members and auditors, this means full accountability.

Threat monitoring and response

Continuous monitoring with defined incident response. For everyone, this means issues are found and handled fast.

Secure development

Security reviews and testing built into how T57 is built. For members, this reduces risk from the start.

Use case · Illustrative example

A Riyadh importer controls who sees its supplier data

A Riyadh food importer adds its finance manager, buyers and an outside customs broker to T57. Each gets a role: buyers see orders and suppliers, the finance manager sees payments, and the customs broker sees only the documents for the shipments assigned to them. The importer's data is hosted in-region, and its admin can review the audit trail at any time.

FAQ

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

T57 is hosted on Microsoft Azure in multiple regions. Where local rules require it, data is stored in-region.

Can other members see my prices or suppliers?

No. Your commercial data is visible only to you and the parties in your trades, according to the permissions you set.

What happens if there is a security incident?

T57 follows a defined incident response process, and affected members and authorities are notified as the law requires.

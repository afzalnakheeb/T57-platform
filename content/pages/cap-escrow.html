T57 solves the risk of paying too early or shipping too soon, by holding buyer funds with a licensed escrow partner and releasing them only when agreed, recorded milestones are met.

The problem

Paying too early or shipping too soon

In food trade, the gap between payment and delivery is where most disputes and losses happen. A buyer pays a deposit and the goods arrive late, short or below grade. A seller ships and the buyer delays payment or disputes quality to renegotiate. Without a neutral party holding the money and clear rules for releasing it, the weaker side usually loses. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Advance-payment fraud

Buyers lose deposits to suppliers who do not deliver, or deliver something else.

Non-payment

Sellers ship and then chase payment for weeks or months.

Quality disputes

Without agreed evidence, arguments about grade or quantity drag on.

How T57 works for you

Agree release terms → Fund escrow → Record milestones → Release or pause

  1. 01

    Agree release terms

    Set milestones and release percentages in the contract, such as deposit on signing, part on inspection and balance on delivery.

  2. 02

    Fund escrow

    The buyer pays into an escrow account held by a licensed partner. The seller sees confirmation before it ships.

  3. 03

    Record milestones

    Inspection reports, bills of lading and delivery confirmations are added to the trade record.

  4. 04

    Release or pause

    Funds release automatically when each milestone is recorded. If either party raises a dispute, release pauses until it is resolved.

Use case · Illustrative example

Karachi rice exporter and Riyadh wholesaler share the risk

A Karachi rice exporter and a verified Riyadh wholesaler are trading for the first time. They agree on T57 to a 20/50/30 escrow: 20% on signing, 50% when pre-shipment inspection confirms grade and weight, and 30% on arrival. The wholesaler funds escrow through T57's licensed partner. The inspection report releases the second payment, and when a few bags arrive damaged, the wholesaler raises a dispute. Only the final 30% pauses, the claim is settled with the insurer, and the balance is released.

FAQ

Common questions

Who holds the money?

A licensed escrow partner, in an account separate from T57 and from both trading parties.

What does escrow cost?

Escrow fees are set by the partner and shown before you agree the contract.

Can escrow be used with a letter of credit or trade finance?

Yes. Escrow can sit alongside an LC or repay a trade finance lender at settlement, on the same trade record.

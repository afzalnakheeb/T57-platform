T57 solves the gap between how food businesses actually order, by message, and the structured records that trade needs, using AI to turn plain language into confirmed orders.

The problem

Orders lost in chat threads

Much of the world's food trade is still ordered by WhatsApp message or phone call: "Send 200 cartons of the same yoghurt on Thursday." It is fast and familiar, but orders get misread, quantities are disputed, and nothing feeds into invoices, stock or finance. Restaurant owners, small retailers and farmers rarely have time to log in to a new system. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Misread orders

Free-text messages lead to wrong products, quantities or delivery dates.

No record

Orders placed by chat do not flow into contracts, invoices or payment records.

Low digital adoption

Many buyers and growers will not use a new app or website for routine orders.

How T57 works for you

Send a message → AI structures it → Confirm → Execute

  1. 01

    Send a message

    Type or speak your order in your own words and language through WhatsApp or T57 chat.

  2. 02

    AI structures it

  3. 03

    Confirm

    You see a clear summary and confirm it, or correct it, before anything is sent.

  4. 04

    Execute

    The confirmed order goes to the supplier and is recorded on the trade record, ready for delivery, invoice and payment.

Use case · Illustrative example

Riyadh restaurant group reorders produce by voice note

The head chef of a Riyadh restaurant group sends a voice note in Arabic: "Same vegetables as Monday, but double the tomatoes, deliver tomorrow morning." T57's AI matches the request to Monday's order with a verified local supplier, doubles the tomatoes and shows a summary. The chef confirms with one tap, the supplier receives a structured order, and the delivery and invoice are recorded automatically.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Verified members can order through WhatsApp or T57 chat once their account is linked.

What if the AI gets my order wrong?

You always see a summary before confirming, so you can correct it. Nothing is sent to the supplier until you confirm.

Is my WhatsApp conversation stored?

Only the order details you confirm are recorded on the trade record, under T57's privacy policy.

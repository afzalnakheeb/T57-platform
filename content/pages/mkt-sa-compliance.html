T57's regulatory guide summarizes the main bodies, documents and rules for food trade in Saudi Arabia, and shows on each trade what is required.

The challenge

The challenge

Food shipments are held at borders every day because of a missing certificate, a wrong label or an unregistered product. Rules differ by country and by product, and they change.

Complex requirements

Different documents for each product and destination.

Changing rules

Requirements are updated often.

Costly mistakes

Errors mean delays, rejections and spoiled goods.

Key bodies and rules in Saudi Arabia

Saudi Food and Drug Authority (SFDA)Regulates food safety, product registration and import requirements.
Halal certificationHalal certificates are required for products such as meat and poultry, from bodies recognized by the SFDA.
Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA)Customs clearance and duties, and electronic invoicing (FATOORA) for VAT-registered businesses.
FASAHThe national single window for import and export procedures.
Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)Governs how personal data is handled.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this guide legal advice?

No. It is a summary to help you prepare. Always confirm requirements with the relevant authority or your customs broker.

How often is the guide updated?

T57 reviews it regularly.

Can T57 handle customs clearance for me?

T57 connects you with licensed customs brokers, who handle clearance on the shared trade record.

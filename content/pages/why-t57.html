The problem

Food trade has plenty of data, but no shared record

Platforms today focus on price discovery and container movements while ignoring the real bottlenecks: fragmented data, a lack of convenient funding, chronic paperwork and policy blind spots.

Governments see too late

They see disconnected snapshots of demand and supply months after the fact, with no live view of waste, surpluses and shocks across borders.

Traders work blind

PDFs, emails and static directories obscure origin, quality and risk, while duplicate audits and changing standards push compliance costs up and price out smallholders.

Producers can't get funded

Small farmers, cooperatives and agri-SMEs face a multi-trillion-dollar financing gap, while logistics, finance and agronomy are still treated as separate worlds.

Why T57

Built where food security, trade and trust meet

A shared digital corridor

Farmers, FPOs, governments, traders and enterprises share one corridor for sourcing, compliance, financing, logistics and settlement, combining verified counterparties, policy-ready infrastructure and real-time market intelligence.

Built with the institutions that shape food security

T57 works with the Islamic Organization for Food Security (IOFS), an OIC institution, under a 10-year memorandum of understanding, and has signed an MoU with ASEAN. That makes T57 a policy-aligned corridor where governments, cooperatives and enterprises can trade with confidence.

FAQ

Common questions

How is T57 different from other food trade platforms?

T57 is an AI-native ecosystem, working with IOFS and ASEAN, that brings trade, finance, logistics and policy-grade data together on one verified platform.

Why do the IOFS and ASEAN partnerships matter to me as a trader?

They translate into faster onboarding, clearer rules of origin and better access to tenders and sovereign buyers, because T57 aligns with the institutions that set food security and trade priorities.

Does T57 only serve governments and institutions?

No. The same rails that serve ministries and multilaterals also serve farmers, cooperatives, SMEs and enterprises, giving every player, big and small, access to verified markets and trusted counterparties.

The challenge

Türkiye is a major producer of fruit, nuts, vegetables, pulses and processed food.

Its agricultural exports exceeded a record $36.4 billion in 2025 (Turkish Exporters Assembly). It sits between Europe, Central Asia and the Middle East, yet many smaller exporters still depend on agents and fragmented paperwork to reach Gulf and Asian buyers. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Reaching new markets

Exporters want direct access to buyers beyond their traditional markets.

Perishable timing

Fresh produce needs fast, accurate documents to clear borders.

Payment risk

New buyers in distant markets bring payment risk.

Use case · Illustrative example

A Mersin citrus exporter enters the Saudi market

A lemon and mandarin exporter in Mersin is matched with a verified supermarket buyer in Jeddah. T57 lists the documents Saudi Arabia requires for fresh citrus, flags a mismatch between the packing list and the phytosanitary certificate before loading, and the buyer pays into escrow through a licensed partner. The exporter ships weekly through the season.

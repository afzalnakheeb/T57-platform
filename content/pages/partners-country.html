FAQ

Common questions

Is this a franchise?

It works like one. You run T57 in an agreed territory under licence, with the T57 brand, platform and support, and share in the revenue from the members you bring.

Which countries are open to partners?

Countries and cities where T57 has no country team yet. See the Global Rollout page for the phases.

Can I cover just one city?

Yes. Territories can be a whole country, a region or a trading city.

Do partners handle money or goods?

No. Members pay through licensed payment and escrow partners, and goods move with logistics partners. Partners do not lend.

Is there a fee to become a partner?

Terms are agreed with each partner and set out in the partner license.

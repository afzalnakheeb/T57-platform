T57 solves the slow, paper-heavy banking that holds food SMEs back, by giving verified members digital business accounts, cards and near-instant settlement from licensed partners.

The problem

Slow, paper-heavy business banking

For many food producers, processors and traders, opening a business account means branch visits, piles of paperwork and weeks of waiting, and some are refused because banks cannot verify them cheaply. Once open, the account knows nothing about the business's trades, so every payment must be matched by hand and every credit request starts from zero.

Slow onboarding

Banks repeat checks that have already been done elsewhere.

Banking blind to trade

Accounts do not see contracts, invoices or deliveries, so banking and trading stay separate.

Limited access for SMEs

Smaller and newer businesses are often under-served or refused.

FAQ

Common questions

Who provides the bank account?

A licensed banking partner in your market, under its own terms and regulation.

Do I have to move my existing banking?

No. You can use T57's banking partners alongside your current bank.

Which countries are covered?

T57's founding markets first (Saudi Arabia, the UAE, India, Malaysia and Türkiye), with more added as partners join.

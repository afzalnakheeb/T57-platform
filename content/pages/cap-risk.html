The problem

One bad shipment from failure

Food trade carries more risk than most. Goods spoil, ships are delayed, buyers default and routes close. When the Strait of Hormuz closed in 2026, FAO warned that 20–45% of key agrifood inputs for some markets pass through it (FAO, April 2026), and fertilizer prices jumped within weeks. Yet many food SMEs ship without adequate insurance, sell on open account without credit cover, and screen counterparties by instinct. One loss can wipe out a year's margin.

Cargo loss and damage

Spoilage, contamination, theft and accidents in transit.

Buyer default

Unpaid invoices and insolvent customers, especially in new markets.

Compliance and disruption risk

Sanctions breaches, route closures and political events that can stop a trade.

Use case · Illustrative example

Jeddah importer's frozen beef shipment is protected end to end

A Jeddah importer buys frozen beef from a verified Brazilian processor. On T57 it insures the cargo, including temperature deviation cover, from a licensed partner. The processor takes trade credit cover on the importer's payment. When a reefer container fails at a transshipment port and part of the cargo is lost, the temperature log, inspection report and policy are already on the trade record, and the claim is settled quickly.

FAQ

Common questions

Does T57 sell insurance?

T57 connects members to licensed insurers, takaful operators and brokers. Policies are issued by them, under their own regulation.

Can I use my existing insurer?

Yes. You can upload existing policies to the trade record so they can be used with LCs, lenders and claims.

What does compliance screening check?

Sanctions lists, watchlists, adverse media and beneficial ownership for every counterparty, before a deal moves forward.

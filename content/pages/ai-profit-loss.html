T57's profit and loss AI solves decisions made on guesswork, by predicting the likely margin of a crop, contract or trade from costs, yields, prices and risks.

The problem without AI

Why this is hard without AI

Many farmers and small food businesses only find out whether a season or a deal made money when it is over. Input costs move fast, as fertilizer prices did after the 2026 Hormuz closure, when urea prices rose about 80% in two months (World Bank, May 2026). Prices at harvest are uncertain, and hidden costs such as freight, finance, losses and payment delays erode margins. Decisions about what to plant, which contract to sign or which buyer to choose are made without a clear view of profit.

How the AI works

Data in, a small local model, a person decides

Model typeScenario models that combine yield forecasts, price forecasts and cost models, with Monte Carlo simulation to show the range of likely outcomes.
Data inputsCrop and yield forecasts. Price index and price forecasts. Input costs (seed, fertilizer, labor, water, equipment rental). Freight, insurance and finance costs. Contract terms and payment timing. The member's own costs, if added.
OutputExpected profit or loss with a likely range, the main drivers of margin, a break-even price, and side-by-side comparisons of scenarios such as different crops, contracts or buyers.

Use case · Illustrative example

Konya wheat and chickpea farmer chooses the better crop

Before

A farmer near Konya planted wheat every year out of habit, even when fertilizer costs rose sharply.

With T57 AI

On T57, the farmer compares wheat with chickpeas for the coming season. With current fertilizer costs, yield forecasts and a contract farming offer from a verified pulses processor, chickpeas show a higher expected margin and a lower break-even price. The farmer plants half the land with chickpeas under contract and secures input finance against it.

FAQ

Common questions

Is the prediction a guarantee?

No. It shows the most likely outcome and a range, based on current data. Actual results depend on weather, markets and execution.

Can I add my own costs?

Yes. Adding your own costs makes predictions more accurate, and your data stays private.

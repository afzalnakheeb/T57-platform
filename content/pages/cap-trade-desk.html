The problem

One or two people doing a whole desk's work

Big trading houses have desks of specialists who source counterparties, track prices, draft contracts, check documents and screen every party for sanctions. Most food SMEs have one or two people doing all of this alongside everything else. The result is slower deals, missed opportunities and costly mistakes, such as a contract that does not match the LC or a counterparty that should never have been approved. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Too few hands

Small teams cannot source, price, draft and check every deal properly.

Compliance exposure

Missing a sanctions hit or a document gap can stop a shipment or a payment and damage a business's reputation with its bank.

Inconsistent paperwork

Contracts and documents prepared by hand often disagree with each other.

Use case · Illustrative example

Izmir dried fruit exporter avoids a costly mismatch

A dried apricot exporter in Izmir agrees to sell to a verified buyer in Doha. The Trade Desk drafts the contract from the accepted quote and screens the buyer and its owners. When the exporter uploads its documents, the desk flags that the packing list shows a different net weight from the contract. The exporter corrects it before presenting documents under the LC, and payment arrives on time instead of being held for a discrepancy.

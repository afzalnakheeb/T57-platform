The challenge

Saudi Arabia is T57's regional headquarters and its largest demand market.

It imports much of its food, and it is also a growing exporter: Saudi date exports reached SAR 1.938 billion in 2025, up 14.3% on the year before, to more than 125 countries (Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture). Yet many small producers and cooperatives still sell through intermediaries and struggle to reach business buyers, export markets and working capital. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Reaching business buyers

Small producers find it hard to sell directly to supermarkets, hotels and institutions.

Secure imports

Importers need verified suppliers and alternative origins when supply is disrupted.

Working capital

Small businesses struggle to finance trade.

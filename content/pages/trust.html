The problem

Food trade still runs on hope that the other side is who they say they are.

Buyers often cannot tell a producer from a reseller. Documents are forged, invoices are financed twice and contracts go unenforced across borders. Banks feel this too: weak data on smaller firms is one reason the Asian Development Bank reports that 41% of SME trade finance applications were rejected in 2025. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Unknown counterparties

Businesses cannot easily confirm who they are trading with, or whether they own what they sell.

Unreliable records

Paper and scattered files make it hard to prove what was agreed and what happened.

Unclear accountability

When a trade goes wrong, there is often no clear process or record to resolve it.

FAQ

Common questions

Is T57 an open marketplace?

No. T57 is a members-only trade club. Every buyer and seller is verified before trading, and verification is renewed.

Does T57 hold my money?

No. Payments, escrow and letters of credit are handled by licensed partners. T57 organizes the trade and records each step.

How do I know who I am trading with?

Every member has a verified T57 Passport that shows their role, checks passed and trade history, shared with consent.

What happens if a trade goes wrong?

T57 provides a structured dispute process based on the shared trade record, and escrow can pause release while a dispute is resolved.

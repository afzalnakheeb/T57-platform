TodayWith T57

Finance, escrow and insurance come from licensed partners.

T57's analytics AI solves the backward-looking reports that leave food businesses reacting late, by turning trade, payment and market data into forecasts and recommended actions on one dashboard.

The problem without AI

Most food SMEs learn how they are doing from bank balances and month-end spreadsheets.

By the time a margin squeeze, a slow-paying buyer or a supplier problem shows up, it is too late to act. Larger firms have analysts and business intelligence tools. Smaller ones have neither the data in one place nor the time to analyze it.

How the AI works

Data in, a small local model, a person decides

Model typeForecasting and anomaly-detection models on the member's own trade data, combined with T57's market models. A language model writes plain-language summaries.
Data inputsThe member's trades, quotes, contracts, shipments, payments and finance on T57. Price index and forecasts. Supply status and risk signals. Counterparty performance.
OutputA dashboard of key measures (volumes, margins, cash cycle, on-time delivery, payment days), forecasts for the coming months, alerts on unusual patterns, and suggested actions, such as which buyers to move to escrow or which origins to diversify.

Use case · Illustrative example

Dubai food trader spots a cash crunch early

Before

A Dubai food trader discovered a cash shortfall only when it could not pay a supplier, after two buyers paid late in the same month.

With T57 AI

Its T57 dashboard forecasts a cash gap six weeks ahead, flags the two buyers whose payment days are lengthening and suggests receivables finance on their invoices. The trader arranges finance from a partner lender and moves one buyer to escrow terms before the gap appears.

FAQ

No. The dashboard is built from your activity on T57. You can add other data, such as costs, for better forecasts.

Can I share the dashboard with my lender?

Yes. You can share selected views with lenders or investors, with your consent.

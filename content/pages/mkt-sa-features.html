Every T57 capability is available to members in Saudi Arabia, with regulated and physical services delivered by licensed local partners.

The challenge

The challenge

Trading food means dealing with buyers, documents, banks, insurers, inspectors and shippers, often through separate systems. On T57, they all connect to one trade record.

Available in Saudi Arabia

Verified membershipKYC, KYB, site visits and the T57 Passport, by T57's local team.
Trade executionRequests for quote, quotes, contracts and invoices, for domestic and cross-border trade.
Trade financeInvoice financing, buyer credit and letters of credit from licensed Saudi partners.
Escrow and paymentsThrough licensed partners, in SAR and any currency the partner supports.
InsuranceCargo and trade credit cover from licensed insurers.
Logistics and inspectionFreight, forwarding and inspection partners, booked from the trade record.
Documents and complianceDestination requirements and document checks, connected to national systems such as ZATCA where available.
AI intelligencePricing, forecasting, supply status and matching, with models for Saudi regions and crops.
LanguageArabic and English.

FAQ

Common questions

Are all features available in every market?

Yes. The partners that deliver regulated services differ by country.

Can I pay in SAR?

Yes, through licensed payment and escrow partners.

Who provides finance in Saudi Arabia?

Licensed Saudi banks and finance companies. T57 does not lend.

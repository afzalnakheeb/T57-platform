The problem

Every country's priorities are different.

One ministry of agriculture wants its farmers and cooperatives to reach new buyers. Another ministry wants to grow food manufacturing, or develop small businesses. A food-security agency needs early warning of supply shocks. Public procurement needs clean, traceable tenders. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Late information

Food-security decisions made on old, aggregate data.

Excluded producers

Smallholders, cooperatives and small businesses left outside formal trade and finance.

Opaque procurement

Large food tenders exposed to leakage, fraud, delays and quality failures.

Use case · Illustrative example

A ministry of agriculture subsidizes cooperatives into export trade

A ministry of agriculture pays for T57 memberships for 200 date and vegetable cooperatives for their first two years. T57's country team verifies them and trains their staff, and the cooperatives start selling to domestic hotel groups and to importers in other founding markets. The ministry's dashboard shows the number of cooperatives trading, the volume and value of their sales, and the prices they receive.

FAQ

Common questions

How is government pricing set?

By program, based on scope, number of members, markets and modules. Contact our institutional team for a proposal.

Can a ministry pay for its farmers' memberships?

Yes. Governments and development partners can fund memberships and onboarding for farmers, cooperatives and SMEs.

Can foreign suppliers respond to our tenders?

Yes, if you open a tender to them. Every supplier is verified before it can bid.

Use case · Illustrative example

Organic certification provider unlocks export-ready growth

A regional certification firm joined T57 to serve producer groups that grow organic spices for export. Through the platform, the firm matched with an FPO needing certification support to reach premium buyers.

The certification firm defined the scope, milestones, and timelines upfront, then worked with farmers on documentation, internal controls, residue risk management, and audit readiness. Because the engagement is embedded in a broader trade workflow, certification is not a one-off service; it becomes part of a coordinated path to market access, faster buyer approval, and repeat business across adjacent regions.

TodayWith T57

Finance, escrow and insurance come from licensed partners.

T57's blockchain layer solves the problem of trade records that can be altered, duplicated or disputed, by anchoring key records on a ledger and automating payments when agreed milestones are recorded.

The problem

Documents that can be altered, pledged twice and disputed

Trade fraud often comes down to documents: an invoice financed twice, a warehouse receipt for goods that do not exist, a certificate changed after inspection. Disputes drag on because nobody can prove which version of a document is real, or when a milestone happened. Paper and PDFs make this easy to exploit and hard to prevent. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Altered documents

Documents can be changed after the fact without detection.

Double financing

The same invoice or receipt can be pledged with several lenders.

Slow, disputed payments

Payment depends on parties agreeing that milestones were met.

How the blockchain layer works

Record → Anchor → Tokenize → Automate

  1. 01

    Record

    Documents and milestones are stored securely on the T57 trade record.

  2. 02

    Anchor

    A digital fingerprint of each key record is written to a blockchain ledger. Any later change to the document can be detected.

  3. 03

    Tokenize

    Eligible trade documents, such as invoices and warehouse receipts, can be issued as unique tokens linked to their owner and trade.

  4. 04

    Automate

    Smart contracts check recorded milestones, such as an inspection pass or delivery confirmation, and instruct licensed partners to release payments.

Components

7 parts, one platform

Record anchoring

Fingerprints of key records written to the ledger. For auditors and lenders, this proves documents have not changed.

Privacy by design

Only fingerprints go on the ledger, never document contents. For members, this protects commercial data.

Tokenized trade documents

Unique digital tokens for invoices, warehouse receipts and bills of lading. For lenders, this prevents double financing.

Smart-contract milestones

Coded release rules linked to escrow and finance. For sellers, this means faster, automatic payment.

Permissioned access

Only authorized parties can see each trade's records. For regulated partners, this supports compliance.

Electronic trade document readiness

Designed to support electronic trade documents where laws such as the UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Transferable Records apply. For exporters, this prepares for paperless trade.

Verification tools

Partners and authorities can check a document's fingerprint against the ledger. For customs and banks, this means fast authenticity checks.

Use case · Illustrative example

Dubai lender avoids a double-financed invoice

A Dubai finance company receives a request to fund an invoice from a food trader. Because the invoice was issued as a token on T57, the lender can see that it is unique, linked to a verified delivery and not pledged elsewhere. It funds the invoice. When the buyer confirms delivery, a smart-contract milestone instructs the escrow partner to repay the lender automatically.

FAQ

Common questions

Is my trade data public on the blockchain?

No. Only digital fingerprints of key records are anchored. Document contents stay in secure storage, visible only to authorized parties.

Do I need cryptocurrency to use the blockchain layer?

No. The blockchain layer works in the background. Members pay and get paid in normal currencies through licensed partners, unless they choose alternative payments where lawful.

What happens if a milestone is disputed?

The smart contract pauses release, and the dispute is handled under the contract and the partner's rules.

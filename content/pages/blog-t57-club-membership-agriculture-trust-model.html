Building Trust at Scale: Why a Club Membership Model Can Transform Agriculture Markets

Why a curated membership with trade history and ratings reduces disputes.

ANAfzal Hussain Mohammed NakheebFounder, Chairman & Head of Strategy

23 Mar 20263 min read

Not everyone is welcome to the T57 club.

Now, before I get angry comments from annoyed readers—don’t worry, it’s nothing personal! At T57, we simply believe that a trustworthy club is the best way to bring certainty, lasting value, and smiles to everyone in the agricultural food chain. The velvet rope isn’t up to keep out the fun; it’s there to make sure everyone inside the club is having an honest-to-goodness time.

How does a club model grow trust?

Why did T57, an end-to-end ecosystem for market participants—farmers, sellers, buyers, service providers, and governments alike—take the bold decision to be a “members-only” trading platform? It’s simple: The biggest risk in agricultural trading isn’t a bad harvest—it’s a bad deal. Online marketplaces are flooded with unvetted participants, and buyers often navigate a maze of unverifiable suppliers and dead ends. On platforms like Alibaba, RFQs (Requests for Quotations) can generate hundreds of replies, with little assurance that the promised quantities or qualities will be delivered. The result? Missed opportunities and endless stress for serious traders and buyers.

At T57, that changes right at the doorstep. Every applicant (potential member) undergoes a real-world onboarding process: one of our officers visits the farm, chats with local market contacts, inspects the conditions, and thoroughly cross-checks every claim. Add to that rigorous anti-money laundering (AML), banking, and legal checks, and every member ends up 100 percent verified. No easy pass; no shortcuts. The ones on the T57 platform are farmers, producers, sellers, buyers, and traders who take their commitments as seriously as their crops.

How does quality over quantity mean fewer disputes?

Sure, to begin with, we may not have millions of sign-ups—and that’s deliberate. We are happy to begin with several thousand users.

A smaller, cleaner membership means disputes drop dramatically, and credibility increases significantly. Globally, open B2B marketplaces experience significant volumes of failed transactions or unresolved conflicts, resulting in billions of dollars in lost time and revenue. By contrast, a curated club model brings order and transparency. Members at T57 can view one another’s trade history, see ratings similar to those on Booking.com and Uber, and trust the blockchain-backed record of every visit, document, audit, and trade. On T57, we promise real deals, real people, real outcomes.

Standing apart: Verified, transparent, human

Why go to these lengths? Because many of the founders of T57 come from an agricultural and trading background, we understand that trust transforms a risky trade into a rewarding partnership. Trust in a digital trading platform can significantly boost ongoing participation by cutting transaction risks—all because users know that opportunists are not lurking on the platform. As OECD’s report, Digital Opportunities for Trade in the Agriculture and Food Sectors, says, “Digital technologies that enable access to information have the potential to encourage changes in the structure of the value chain.” That is what we are aiming for.

When you see that “T57 Badge,” you know the member has passed every test and trades are not one-offs, but part of a reliable, growing network.

Advanced features, such as blockchain verification, mutual ratings, leaderboards, and transparent performance metrics, are built right into T57’s club membership model. This approach encourages commitment from both sellers and buyers, so you’re never left guessing about delivery, payment, or the real story behind a deal.

Ready for the club? Here’s what it means

T57 isn’t just another platform; it’s a new standard. Think Rotary or Lions Club—entry comes with conditions, but membership is a privilege that pays back every day. For T57, calling it a “club” isn’t about exclusion—it’s about building a foundation strong enough for serious business, where every handshake has the reliability of a contract and every deal is vouched for on the blockchain.

So, who’s welcome? Those who value transparency over shortcuts and partnership over opportunism. And if that sounds like your kind of crowd, there’s a whole new world of opportunity waiting for you on the inside.​

For those who get past the entry door—welcome to T57, where trust is what we grow and trust is what we harvest, together.

Don’t wait; become part of the T57 community — your membership starts here.

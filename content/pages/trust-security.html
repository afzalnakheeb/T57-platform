TodayWith T57

Finance, escrow and insurance come from licensed partners.

T57 exists to give food businesses a place to trade where their prices, partners and documents are protected, and where they decide who sees what.

The problem

Food businesses guard their buyers, suppliers and margins closely.

Sharing them on a platform feels risky, and many digital tools give little control over who sees data or how it is used. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Fear of exposure

Prices and partners leaking to competitors.

Loss of control

Not knowing who has seen or used your data.

Account takeover

Stolen passwords used to redirect payments or sign deals.

How T57 protects you

Secure access → Your rules → Protected storage → Full visibility

Features

What is included

Multi-factor sign-inFor every user. For members, this stops stolen passwords being enough.
Role-based permissionsSet by company, role and trade. For business owners, this keeps control in your hands.
Consent controlsYour Passport, trade history and rating are shared only when you choose. For members, this means privacy by default.
Confidential pricingYour prices are visible only to the parties you quote. For sellers, this protects margins.

Use case · Illustrative example

A Dubai trader shares only what each partner needs

A UAE trading house shares its full Passport with a partner bank for finance, shares only its verification status and rating with a new supplier in India, and keeps its buying prices private from everyone except the sellers it quotes. Its admin reviews the access history each month.

FAQ

Common questions

Does T57 sell my data?

No. T57 does not sell members' individual data. Market insights use aggregated, anonymized data.

Can T57 staff see my data?

Only staff who need it for verification, support or compliance, under strict access controls, and access is logged.

Can I delete my data?

You can ask for deletion, subject to records the law requires T57 or its partners to keep.

How do I report a security concern?

Contact our security team at hello@t57.ai.

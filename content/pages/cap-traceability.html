T57 solves the problem of food whose origin and journey cannot be proven, by linking every lot to its source, processing, shipment and documents on a tamper-evident record.

The problem

Origin and journey that cannot be proven

Buyers, regulators and consumers increasingly want to know exactly where food comes from: which farm or fishery, which processor, whether it is halal, organic or deforestation-free, and how it was stored and shipped. Large importers and retailers now ask suppliers for this proof as a condition of buying. Most food SMEs keep this information on paper or in separate systems, so they cannot answer quickly or convincingly, and a single food safety incident can lead to a costly, oversized recall.

Origin claims you cannot prove

Certificates and declarations are easy to copy and hard to check.

Slow, broad recalls

Without lot-level records, a problem in one batch can force a recall of far more product.

Market access at risk

Buyers and regulators may refuse goods that lack traceability records.

How T57 works for you

FAQ

Common questions

Who adds information to a lot's record?

The parties who handle it, such as the producer, processor, inspector and logistics partner, each add the events they are responsible for.

Can consumers see traceability information?

Yes, if the brand or seller chooses to share it through a link or QR code. Commercial details stay private.

Does traceability on T57 replace certification?

No. Certificates are still issued by accredited bodies. T57 links them to the lots they cover and makes them easy to check.

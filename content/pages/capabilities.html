The problem

Eight services, eight copies of the deal

A single food trade still needs eight separate services. Someone has to find and check the counterparty, agree the price and terms, arrange finance, protect the payment, insure the cargo, inspect the goods, ship them and clear customs. Today each is bought separately, with its own paperwork and its own view of the deal. Details are retyped, documents drift out of line, and nobody sees the whole trade. That is why banks rejected 41% of SME trade finance applications in 2025 (ADB, January 2026), and why so many good food trades stall between agreement and delivery. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Disconnected services

Finance, insurance, inspection and logistics providers each work from their own copy of the deal.

Repeated work

The same product, price and party details are typed again and again, and every retyping risks an error.

No single view

Buyers, sellers, lenders and regulators cannot see where a trade stands or trust that its records are complete.

Use case · Illustrative example

Indian processor runs an export from quote to payment

A rice processor in Haryana receives an RFQ from a verified Riyadh distributor. It quotes, the distributor accepts, and the contract is generated from the quote. The distributor's bank issues an electronic LC through T57, cargo insurance and pre-shipment inspection are booked from the same record, and a freight partner moves the goods to Jeddah with documents ready for customs. The processor tracks every step in one place and is paid on compliant presentation, without retyping a single detail.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to use every capability on every trade?

No. Members choose the capabilities each trade needs. Because they share one record, adding a service later does not mean starting again.

Who provides finance, payments and insurance?

Licensed partners. T57 does not lend, hold client funds, own goods or provide transport. It provides the verification, workflow and trade record that connect these services.

Can I use T57 for domestic trade as well as exports?

Yes. T57 supports domestic wholesale to supermarkets, HoReCa and institutions, as well as cross-border trade. See domestic B2B trade.

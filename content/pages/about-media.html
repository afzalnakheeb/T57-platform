T57 at a glance

Fact sheet

What it is
AI-native digital trade infrastructure for the entire food ecosystem.
Model
A members-only network of verified businesses trading on one record, with finance, insurance, logistics and inspection from licensed partners.
Founding markets
Saudi Arabia, the UAE, India, Malaysia and Türkiye.
Headquarters
6709 King Fahad Street, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. T57 started in Madinah Al Munawwarah.
Founder and Chairman
Afzal Hussain Mohammed Nakheeb.
Institutional relationship
10-year MoU with the Islamic Organization for Food Security (IOFS), since June 2023.

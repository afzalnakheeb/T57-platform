The challenge

Buying from suppliers you cannot fully check

Food importers carry the most risk in a trade. They often pay before goods arrive, rely on documents they cannot easily verify and deal with suppliers they have never met. The stakes are rising: the global food import bill is forecast at about $2.22 trillion for 2025 (FAO Food Outlook, November 2025), and in 2026 the closure of the Strait of Hormuz showed how quickly supply routes into the Gulf can be disrupted.

Supplier uncertainty

It is hard to tell whether a supplier is the producer, a processor or one more intermediary adding cost.

Quality and documentation risk

Certificates, inspection reports and contracts arrive from different sources and do not always match.

Payment and supply risk

Paying upfront exposes buyers to fraud and delay, while single-source supply leaves them exposed to shocks.

Use case · Illustrative example

Kuala Lumpur distributor sources dates from a Madinah producer

A Kuala Lumpur food distributor wants premium dates ahead of Ramadan but has been let down by intermediaries before. On T57 it filters for suppliers labeled owner-producer and finds a verified family date farm in Madinah. It sends an RFQ, compares quotes against price data and accepts one. The contract is generated from the quote, a partner bank issues an LC and an inspection partner checks grade and halal documents before loading. The distributor tracks the shipment on one trade record and pays only when agreed conditions are met.

FAQ

Common questions

How does T57 verify suppliers?

T57 runs business and identity checks, compliance screening and, where needed, a physical check of the supplier's premises. Each verified supplier receives a T57 Passport and a role label.

Can I buy only from producers?

Yes. Role labels show whether a supplier is an owner-producer, processor, trader or intermediary, so you can filter for producers.

Does T57 hold my payment?

No. T57 does not hold client funds. Escrow and letters of credit are provided by licensed partners.

Can I buy locally as well as import?

Yes. T57 supports domestic wholesale for supermarkets, HoReCa and institutions as well as cross-border trade. See domestic B2B trade.

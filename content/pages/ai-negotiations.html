TodayWith T57

Finance, escrow and insurance come from licensed partners.

T57's negotiation AI solves the information gap between buyers and sellers, by preparing briefs, benchmarking offers and suggesting counter-offers that weigh price, payment, delivery and quality together.

The problem without AI

In food deals, the better-informed side usually wins.

A small exporter facing a large importer often does not know the going price for its grade, what longer payment terms really cost, or how far it can push. Many accept the first offer. Others lose good deals by pushing too hard. Both outcomes cost money, and neither side can see how the final deal compares with the market.

How the AI works

Data in, a small local model, a person decides

Model typeA pricing and deal-value model built on the T57 price index and recorded trade outcomes, combined with a language model that writes briefs and explanations. Regional models handle local terms and languages.
Data inputsThe current quote or offer. The T57 price index and similar anonymized trades. Freight, finance and insurance cost estimates. The counterparty's public T57 profile and track record. Your own costs and margins, if you choose to add them.
OutputA private brief with the market range, a suggested target, opening and walk-away points, the value of each term (price, payment days, Incoterms, delivery) and suggested counter-offers, with reasons. Nothing is sent without your confirmation.

Use case · Illustrative example

Kayseri dried apricot exporter values payment terms correctly

Before

A dried apricot exporter in Kayseri accepted a Dubai buyer's offer at a good price, without realizing that 120-day payment terms wiped out most of its margin once financing costs were counted.

With T57 AI

On T57, the brief shows the offer's price is above the market median, but the payment terms reduce its real value below the index. The engine suggests a counter-offer at a slightly lower price with escrow released on arrival. The buyer agrees, and the exporter's margin is protected.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the AI negotiate on my behalf?

No. It prepares and suggests. You decide every offer and acceptance.

Can both parties use the engine in the same deal?

Yes. Each party gets its own private brief based on the same public market data.

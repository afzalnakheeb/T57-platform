The problem

Paper letters of credit: slow and error-prone

The letter of credit is one of the oldest and safest ways to pay for goods across borders, but it is slow. Applications are filled in by hand, documents are couriered between banks, and small differences between the LC and the shipping documents lead to rejections and delays. For food, where goods spoil and prices move, every day spent waiting is a cost. Many smaller exporters and importers avoid LCs altogether and take on more risk instead.

FAQ

Common questions

What does "hours, not weeks" mean?

It is the target time for a partner bank to issue the electronic LC after your application has been approved. Approval itself follows the bank's own credit process.

Does T57 issue letters of credit?

No. Letters of credit are issued by licensed partner banks. T57 prepares the application from your trade record and connects you to them.

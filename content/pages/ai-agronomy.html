TodayWith T57

T57's agronomy AI solves the shortage of expert advice for farmers, by answering questions instantly in local languages and connecting farmers to verified agronomists for the problems AI cannot solve alone.

The problem without AI

Most smallholders have little or no access to agronomists.

When a crop shows signs of disease, pests or nutrient stress, farmers rely on neighbors, input sellers or guesswork, and often act too late or use the wrong treatment. Extension services are stretched thin, and advice is rarely available in the farmer's own language, at the moment it is needed.

How the AI works

Data in, a small local model, a person decides

Model typeSmall, local language and vision models for each region and crop. They answer questions and identify likely problems from photos, and are trained with agronomists and on recorded farmer knowledge.
Data inputsThe farmer's question by voice, text or photo. Crop, variety and growth stage. Local weather, soil and pest alerts. Agronomic guidance validated by experts. Farmer knowledge recorded with consent.
OutputA plain-language answer in the farmer's language with suggested next steps and a confidence level. Serious or uncertain cases go straight to a verified agronomist.

Use case · Illustrative example

Sindh mango grower saves a harvest

Before

A mango grower in Sindh saw black spots on fruit and sprayed what a local shop recommended, without effect, and lost part of the crop.

With T57 AI

The grower sends photos and a voice note in Sindhi through T57. The AI identifies a likely fungal disease with medium confidence and passes the case to a verified agronomist, who confirms it the same day and recommends a treatment approved for export markets. The harvest meets the buyer's quality standard.

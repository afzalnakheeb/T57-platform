T57's Know Your Business (KYB) checks confirm that every member company is real, properly registered, licensed and owned by the people it names.

The problem

A company name and a website prove very little.

Shell companies, borrowed licences and hidden owners are common routes to fraud and sanctions breaches in cross-border trade. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Shell and fake companies

Businesses that exist only on paper.

Hidden ownership

Owners who are not disclosed, or who are sanctioned.

Expired licences

Export, food-safety or trading licences that have lapsed.

How KYB works

Submit → Check → Screen → Approve and monitor

  1. 01

    Submit

    The business uploads registration documents, licences, ownership details and bank information.

  2. 02

    Check

    T57 confirms registration, directors and beneficial owners against official registries where available, and checks document authenticity.

  3. 03

    Screen

    The company and its owners are screened against sanctions and watchlists.

  4. 04

    Approve and monitor

    An analyst approves the file, and licences are tracked with expiry reminders and periodic re-checks.

FAQ

Common questions

What documents do I need for KYB?

Company registration, licences, details of directors and owners, and bank information. The exact list depends on your country and role.

Who sees my KYB documents?

T57's verification team. Others see only your verified Passport, and only what you choose to share.

How often is KYB renewed?

Periodically, and whenever key details such as ownership or licences change.

Can a cooperative pass KYB on behalf of its farmers?

Yes. A cooperative or producer organization is verified as a business, and its member producers are onboarded through it.

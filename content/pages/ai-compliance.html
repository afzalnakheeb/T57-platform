The problem without AI

Why this is hard without AI

Verifying a business properly means checking registration documents, licenses, owners, bank details and sanctions lists, and repeating parts of it for every trade. Done by hand, it takes days or weeks, varies from reviewer to reviewer and still misses things. For a members-only trade club, weak checks would undermine trust. Checks that are too slow would keep good businesses out.

How the AI works

Data in, a small local model, a person decides

Model typeDocument understanding models that read and extract data, entity matching for owners and companies, and risk models for screening. All of them work inside a human review workflow.
Data inputsCompany documents, licenses, owners and bank details submitted by the applicant. Business registries. Sanctions lists, watchlists and adverse media. Physical verification reports. Ongoing trade activity on T57.
OutputExtracted, cross-checked application data, document authenticity flags, ownership and screening results, and a recommendation to an analyst. Analysts make the final decision, and every adverse decision is reviewed by a person.

Use case · Illustrative example

Chennai food processor joins T57

Before

A Chennai food processor spent three weeks sending documents to a bank and a buyer for separate checks.

With T57 AI

It applies to T57 and uploads its documents once. The AI extracts and cross-checks the data, matches the owners against registries, screens everyone against sanctions lists and flags one expired license. The processor uploads the renewed license, an analyst approves the application after a physical check, and the T57 Passport is issued. With consent, it is shared with a partner bank to speed up account opening.

FAQ

Common questions

Can AI reject my application?

No. AI can flag issues, but only a person can make an adverse decision, and you can ask for a review.

How is my data protected?

Your data is used for verification and shared with partners only with your consent, under T57's privacy policy and applicable data protection laws.

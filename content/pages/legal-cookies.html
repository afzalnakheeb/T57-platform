No tracking. No ad cookies.

What this website stores in your browser, and what it never does.

This page explains how t57.ai uses cookies and similar browser storage. The short answer: the site sets no tracking or advertising cookies, so we do not need to ask for consent before you browse.

What the site stores in your browser

NameTypePurposeHow long
t57-goSession storageContinues the page transition when you move from one section to another.Until you close the tab
t57-seenSession storagePlays the opening logo animation once per visit, not on every page.Until you close the tab
t57-toneLocal storageRemembers the display setting you choose.Until you clear it

These items stay in your browser. They are not sent to us, and they contain no personal data.

What the site does not use

  • No analytics cookies or analytics scripts.
  • No advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • No social-media pixels or embedded tracking.

Fonts and every other file on the site are served from t57.ai itself, so loading a page does not send your IP address to a third party. Our hosting provider sees standard request data, as explained in the Privacy Notice.

How to control browser storage

You can clear or block site storage in your browser settings. The site still works if you do; page transitions and the opening animation simply play differently.

Changes

If we ever add cookies that need your consent, such as analytics, we will ask before setting them and update this page first.

The problem

Equipment too costly to own, idle when owned

Small farms produce around a third of the world's food (FAO, 2021), but few smallholders can afford to buy a tractor, harvester or cold room that they use only a few weeks a year. Without the right equipment at the right time, harvests are late, quality drops and more food is lost. Meanwhile, equipment owners, from larger farms to service companies, have machines standing idle for much of the year. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Unaffordable equipment

Buying machinery is out of reach for most small farmers and cooperatives.

Bad timing

Without equipment at harvest, crops are picked late or lost.

Idle assets

Equipment owners lack a trusted way to rent machines to people they do not know.

Use case · Illustrative example

Punjab farmer cooperative rents harvesters for the wheat season

A farmer producer organization in Punjab needs combine harvesters for a two-week wheat harvest window. On T57 it finds two verified owners nearby and books both machines with operators. Payment and a deposit are held in escrow by a licensed partner. Condition is recorded at handover, the harvest is completed on time, and the cooperative's buyer, under a contract farming agreement, receives grain at the agreed quality.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I list my equipment for rent?

Yes. Verified members can list equipment, set prices and availability, and choose who can rent it.

Who is responsible if equipment is damaged?

The rental terms, condition records and any insurance set out responsibility. Disputes are handled under those terms and the payment partner's rules.

Is equipment rental available everywhere?

It starts in T57's founding markets and grows as owners join in each region.

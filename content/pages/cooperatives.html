The challenge

Collective strength, without the market power to match

Cooperatives and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) exist to protect their members by securing better prices, but they often operate with limited resources in highly competitive markets.

Many cooperatives still run on manual processes, unable to achieve true scale

Many struggle with professional management. For cooperatives & FPOs, this often means slow decision-making and difficulty presenting themselves as contract-ready partners to large buyers and governments.

FPOs struggle to deliver full value to members

Many FPOs lack consistent market access, modern finance, and the tools needed to operate as professional agribusiness clusters, limiting their ability to translate aggregation into long-term income gains for farmers.

Weak bargaining power and underused local brands

Many FPOs sell into commodity channels, while their own local brands remain underdeveloped; they are not leveraging their member base to win larger contracts or tender-based business.

Use case · Illustrative example

Indian FPO scales local brand through contract-backed exports

A vegetable FPO in Maharashtra, India, aggregates produce from more than 800 smallholder members and has built a modest local brand, but it primarily sells to regional wholesalers at fluctuating spot prices. Despite its infrastructure and membership, the FPO struggles to secure institutional contracts or access export buyers because it lacks market intelligence, tendering experience, and structured finance.

After onboarding to T57, the FPO publishes its seasonal production plan and uses AI-driven trade matchmaking to identify buyers seeking traceable vegetables. Through RFQ bidding, it responds to a supermarket chain's regional sourcing tender and a Gulf-based importer's contract opportunity, with support from T57's virtual trade desk. With trade finance from partner banks, the FPO secures pre-shipment funding, routes payments through escrow, and uses T57's document and logistics tools to manage export-ready consignments. Over two seasons, its members experience more predictable prices, improved cash flow, and stronger demand for the FPO's own brand.

FAQ

Common questions

Why do cooperatives & FPOs need digital platforms like T57?

Scale alone is not enough. Cooperatives and FPOs need systems that professionalize operations, improve market access, and strengthen bargaining power. When properly supported and organized by platforms like T57, agricultural cooperatives play a critical role in sustainable agrifood systems and food security.

How do members gain when their cooperative or FPO wins government tenders or a contract farming agreement?

Government tenders and contract farming agreements lock in demand and timelines, so members know what to produce, when it will be picked up, and at what terms, which leads to more consistent prices and lower post-harvest losses. T57 helps members plan volumes and crop mix, match supply to tender requirements, coordinate logistics and documentation, and track fulfillment and payments in one place, turning tender wins and contracts into a distributed benefit for individual farmers.

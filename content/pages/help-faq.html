FAQ

Common questions

How long does the free trial last?

Founding members get 90 days free at launch. After the launch period, the trial is 14 days.

Can I trade during the free trial?

You can explore members, intelligence and AI tools, and send requests for quote. Contracts, escrow and finance start once you subscribe and are verified.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

From your account settings. Upgrades apply at once, and downgrades apply at renewal.

Does T57 hold my money?

No. Payments, escrow and letters of credit are handled by licensed partners.

Can other members see my prices?

No. Only the parties you quote can see your prices.

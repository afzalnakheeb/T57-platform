The problem without AI

Why this is hard without AI

A single food shipment needs a contract, pro forma and commercial invoices, a packing list, a certificate of origin request, LC documents, an insurance declaration and customs forms. The same data is retyped into each one by different people. A single wrong weight, date or description causes LC discrepancies, customs holds or disputes, and in food, delays mean spoiled goods and lost money.

FAQ

Common questions

Are AI-generated contracts legally binding?

Contracts become binding when the parties sign them. T57 uses standard trade terms, and members can have their own lawyers review any document.

Which documents can be generated?

Contracts, pro forma and commercial invoices, packing lists, LC applications and supporting documents, and data for certificates and customs declarations.

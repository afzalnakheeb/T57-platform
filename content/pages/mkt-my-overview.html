The challenge

Malaysia is a global halal hub.

Its halal exports reached RM68.52 billion in 2025, up 10.9%, and food and beverages made up RM36.86 billion of that (Halal Development Corporation). Yet many small and mid-sized makers still find it hard to reach buyers in the Gulf and South Asia directly, or to finance large orders. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Reaching distant buyers

SMEs rely on agents and trade shows to find importers.

Proving halal and quality

Buyers want certificates they can verify.

Financing growth

Larger export orders need working capital.

Use case · Illustrative example

A Sabah seafood processor ships to Dubai

A frozen seafood processor in Sabah, with a JAKIM halal certificate and an HACCP certificate recorded on its T57 Passport, is matched with a verified Dubai importer. The importer pays through a letter of credit from a partner bank, and the processor tracks the cold-chain shipment and documents on one record.

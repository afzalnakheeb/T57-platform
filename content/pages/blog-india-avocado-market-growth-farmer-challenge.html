Bridging the Gap: How T57 Can Transform India’s Emerging Avocado Industry

India’s fast-growing avocado imports, and the gaps growers face.

ABAbdul Bari KareemCo-founder & CTO

17 Mar 20263 min read

India's avocado story represents one of agriculture's most dramatic transformations. As Rahul Mathur, a pre-seed investor, recently highlighted on X, imports have surged 50-fold over the past five years, reaching approximately 12,000 tons in FY25—a trend that continued into 2025, with imports hitting 7,600 MT between January and May alone. Yet beneath this growth lies a constellation of challenges that T57 is uniquely positioned to solve through its comprehensive farm-to-table ecosystem.

Hass, Pinkerton, or Arka Coorg Ravi?

When Ananda SN responded to Mathur’s post, expressing interest in cultivating avocados on three acres near Bangalore, he articulated the central problem farmers face: "There are a lot of unknowns in this variety that is adaptable to the weather and soil conditions, market to sell in bulk, prices during peak cultivation season". This uncertainty represents a critical inflection point where expert guidance can determine the success or failure of a harvest.

T57's network of agri experts precisely addresses this gap. With a deep understanding of soil health and crop management, these specialists can guide individuals like Ananda in selecting varietals—whether to pursue the premium Hass variety or Pinkerton, which has an American origin, or Indian varieties such as Arka Coorg Ravi. Their expertise extends to addressing the specific challenges that @nikhilaha identified in the conversation started by Mathur: the seasonal nature of Coorg production (August-September only), shelf-life considerations, and the quality differentials that affect market pricing.

What is the best way to align supply with demand in the agri-industry?

Rahul's observation that "There is a LOT of production activity taking place in India" around avocados signals an imminent supply expansion. However, as Tanzania's 2025 market glut demonstrated—where diverted European volumes flooded India, destabilizing prices—uncoordinated production can backfire catastrophically. T57's ecosystem provides the crucial AI-driven market intelligence layer, offering real-time demand forecasting with pricing trends.

The T57 platform's value becomes especially apparent when considering the complex and sophisticated market structure for avocados, which includes premium imports from South Africa through players like Westfalia Fruit, domestic production partnerships across the Western Ghats, and even experimental cultivation by Tata Coffee. A platform like T57 is designed to position emerging farmers within this complex landscape, ensuring they understand competition, market timing, quality premiums, and buyer requirements before committing resources.

Can technological solutions add to conventional wisdom?

Beyond conventional wisdom, T57 integrates access to modern agricultural expertise—from precision irrigation systems to data-driven crop selection. For avocado cultivation, which demands specific soil pH, drainage conditions, and climatic parameters, such support can mean the difference between thriving orchards and failed investments.

As India moves to replace a significant portion of its avocado imports with domestic production over the next decade, platforms like T57 provide the bridge between aspiring cultivators and market success. By providing tailored solutions that address variety selection, cultivation techniques, market access, and pricing intelligence, T57 transforms agricultural uncertainty into calculated opportunity—precisely what farmers like Ananda need to confidently enter this promising sector.

The problem

Open marketplaces: who is real?

Without a club-based model, food and agri trade is opaque and unreliable. Traditionally, buyers have posted RFQs on marketplaces and waited days, only to receive incomplete or non-serious responses, and never knowing who is behind a profile. Sellers have struggled to distinguish genuine demand from trial inquiries, resulting in delayed or failed payments that have eroded margins and trust.

T57 changes this by determining each member's trust score through a blockchain-enabled process that evaluates document quality, business legitimacy, compliance and risk, on-field verification, trade history, financial stability, reference response, partner relationship, ecosystem reputation, and overall reputation. The evaluation is used to generate a verified T57 ID with appropriate badges and a score, giving farmers and producers, cooperatives and FPOs, buyers and importers, sellers and exporters, traders and aggregators, manufacturers and enterprises, food processors, service providers, financiers and investors, partners and collaborators, and governments and regulators a stronger basis for reducing counterparty and transaction risk.

Use case · Illustrative example

When trusted capacity really matters

A mid-sized, export-focused food processor in North Africa seeks to bid on a multi-year government tender for fortified wheat-based products but cannot, on its own, meet the required annual volume or the stringent delivery performance clauses. Through T57 Club Membership, the processor can access a set of pre-verified upstream mills and aggregators with documented volume histories, financial standing, and quality records.

Using this visibility, the processor forms a virtual consortium of club members whose combined, verified capacity meets the tender requirement, enabling a credible bid backed by traceable supply, predictable lead times, and known payment behavior. Because all parties are already KYC/KYB-cleared and trade on the same platform, the government buyer gains greater confidence in the continuity of supply and compliance, and participating members gain access to high-quality demand that would be inaccessible in an open, uncurated marketplace.

FAQ

Common questions

How does the T57 trust score help farmers and producers, cooperatives, and FPOs reduce risk?

The trust score helps these groups assess whether prospective buyers, aggregators, processors, or service partners have the legitimacy, financial stability, trade history, and relationship strength needed to support dependable transactions. This reduces the risk of engaging with weak or opaque counterparties.

Why is the T57 ID useful for financiers and investors, partners, collaborators, governments, and regulators?

The T57 ID provides these stakeholders with a structured view of whether an organization has been verified across key trust factors, including compliance, field validation, references, and ecosystem reputation. That makes it easier to evaluate risk and engage with businesses whose credentials are supported by a more advanced process than standard marketplace profiles or basic business identifiers.

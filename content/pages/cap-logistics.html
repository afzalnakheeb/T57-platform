The problem

Food spoils, stalls at ports or arrives late

Around 13.3% of the world's food is lost between harvest and retail, much of it in transport, storage and handling (FAO, 2023). For food businesses, logistics is where agreed trades go wrong. They book freight by phone and email, chase shipment updates through several forwarders, and see goods held at ports because a document is missing or inconsistent. Cold chain failures and customs delays turn profit into loss, and nobody has the full picture. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Fragmented booking

Freight, cold storage, inspection and customs are arranged separately, with different providers and paperwork.

No visibility

Buyers and sellers do not know where goods are or when they will arrive.

Delays at the border

Missing or mismatched documents hold perishable goods at ports.

Use case · Illustrative example

Agadir tomato exporter keeps a reefer shipment on time to Dubai

A tomato exporter in Agadir sells to a verified Dubai distributor on CFR terms. Through T57 it compares reefer quotes from vetted forwarders, books inspection at the packhouse and generates export documents from the contract. The distributor follows the container's position and temperature on the same record. Import documents are lodged before arrival, the cargo clears quickly, and delivery confirmation releases the final escrow payment.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I use my own freight forwarder?

Yes. You can invite your forwarder to the trade record or upload its documents and tracking data.

Does T57 support cold-chain shipments?

Yes. Cold-chain partners and temperature tracking are available for chilled and frozen goods.

Which modes of transport are covered?

Sea, air and road freight, plus warehousing and cold storage, through vetted partners.

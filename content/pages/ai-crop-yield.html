T57's crop AI solves the uncertainty that makes farmers hard to finance and supply hard to plan, by forecasting yields and harvest timing field by field, using local models and what farmers know.

The problem without AI

Why this is hard without AI

Small farms produce around a third of the world's food (FAO, 2021), but their output is largely invisible until harvest. Farmers cannot prove what they will produce, so lenders hesitate. Buyers cannot plan how much they will receive. Cooperatives aggregate by guesswork. Weather is becoming less predictable, which makes old rules of thumb less reliable every season.

How the AI works

Data in, a small local model, a person decides

Model typeSmall, local crop models for each region and crop, combining satellite-based crop monitoring, weather-driven growth models and machine learning.
Data inputsSatellite images of the field. Weather history and forecasts. Soil and water data. Planting dates and varieties. Past yields. Farmer observations recorded by voice or photo in their own language, with consent.
OutputA yield forecast with a range for each field, cooperative or region, an expected harvest window, and alerts when crop conditions change.

Use case · Illustrative example

Andhra Pradesh chilli cooperative plans its season

Before

A chilli farmer producer organization in Guntur estimated its harvest by asking members, often overpromising to buyers and then missing volumes.

With T57 AI

On T57, each member's field is monitored, and farmers add notes by voice in Telugu. Mid-season, the model forecasts a lower yield in one district after a dry spell. The cooperative adjusts its contract farming volumes with its buyer early, avoids a shortfall penalty and plans irrigation where it helps most.

FAQ

Common questions

Do farmers need special equipment?

No. Forecasts use satellite and weather data. Farmers can add observations by voice or photo from a basic smartphone, or through their cooperative.

Who can see a farm's forecast?

The farmer or cooperative, and anyone they choose to share it with, such as a buyer or lender.

Three ways T57 reaches a market

Local teams, online access and country partners

01 · Founding markets

A local team, in person

In Saudi Arabia, the UAE, India, Malaysia and Türkiye, a dedicated country team handles sales, onboarding, physical verification and member support.

02 · Anywhere, from day one

Open online to every country

A business in any country can apply online. Digital KYB and KYC checks and compliance screening come first, then it trades with verified members. Local services depend on partner coverage.

How T57 scales

Local presence that matches each market

T57 does not build a large office in every country. As the footprint grows, onboarding, verification and language support move to regional hubs, planned for Bangalore, Cairo and Casablanca, and local presence is sized to each market.

Full country teamsIn the largest markets: sales, onboarding, physical verification and member support on the ground.

FAQ

Common questions

Is T57 only for the five founding markets?

No. The five founding markets have country teams on the ground, but T57 is open online to verified businesses in 195 countries, and new markets open phase by phase.

Can I join if my country is not a T57 market yet?

Yes. Apply online and complete digital KYB and KYC checks and compliance screening. Some local services, such as delivery or pay-later terms, depend on partner coverage in your country.

What changes when T57 opens a market?

A local team or country partner, local-language support, local logistics, inspection and finance partners, and a guide to that country's food rules.

How does T57 choose the next markets?

By trade corridors with real supply and demand, partner readiness through IOFS and ASEAN, legal opinions and demand from members. A phase opens only when these are in place.

Are the dates fixed?

No. They are targets from T57's rollout plan, and each phase opens when it is ready.

What is a T57 country partner?

A licensed local business that runs T57 in its country or city, like a franchise. See the Country Partner page to apply.

Why pricing matters

Food businesses already pay for trade in many hidden ways: agents' margins, repeated document checks, bank charges, failed shipments and time spent chasing partners. Many platforms add listing fees or charge for leads that never become trades. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Paying for nothing

One size for all

Pricing that ignores the size of the business and the market it works in.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I pay to list products or send quotes?

No. Listing, quoting and negotiating are included in your membership. The trade fee applies only when a trade is completed.

Does T57 earn anything from finance or insurance?

Yes. T57 earns a small commission from some finance and insurance partners. It is disclosed before you accept a partner's offer.

Are prices the same in every country?

The prices shown are standard list prices in US dollars. Local prices may be lower in markets with lower purchasing power. Contact us for pricing in your country.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Founding members get 90 days free at launch. The trial lets you explore the network and tools. Trading starts once you subscribe and are verified.

The people building T57.

The founders, leadership team, board and advisors behind T57, with experience in food trade, food security, technology, finance and governance.

Meet the foundersJoin our team

T57 is led by five founders with decades of experience in food trade, technology, product and governance. They are joined by leaders for each market and function, and guided by a board of thirteen advisors.

Founders

Five founders

Afzal Hussain Mohammed NakheebFounder, Chairman and Head of StrategyLinkedInafzalnakheeb.com

Afzal works where food security, trade and public policy meet. Over three decades he has built food, trade and enterprise systems across Saudi Arabia, the UAE and India. At T57 he leads strategy and the platform's partnerships with governments, multilateral institutions and development finance partners.

As Founder and Chairman of Al Mukarramah, he led the team that designed the strategy and ecosystem of the Islamic Organization for Food Security (IOFS), and in 2023 he signed a ten-year strategic implementation agreement with the organization. Since then he has signed memoranda of understanding with the Government of the Chechen Republic (2025), the Development Bank of Maldives (2026) and Kazakhstan's Food Contract Corporation (2026). He is also Chairman of AMK Holding, a Riyadh-based multi-family office that aligns patient capital with long-term economic programs.

In Madinah, as Vice President of Namaa Al Munawara, he built the Made in Madinah ecosystem for local producers, which enabled more than 3,000 jobs and trained more than 700 Saudi women. He served as Chief Operating Officer of BMPC, the agribusiness founded by Sheikh Saleh Kamel. Earlier in his career he ran FMCG distribution for global brands in Dubai and hypermarket operations in India.

Afzal speaks on food security, AI and trade finance at LEAP, Money20/20 Middle East, the Gulfood World Economy Summit, KazanForum and the China–Arab States Expo. He holds an MBA in Retail Management and has completed executive programs at Harvard Business School, Stanford and Babson College. He works in English, Arabic, Hindi and Kannada.

Abdul Bari KareemCo-founder and CTOLinkedIn

Abdul Bari builds and scales enterprise software for global clients. He has overseen SAP, Oracle and related transformation programs for blue-chip organizations, and has led engineering teams delivering high-availability platforms under strict security and compliance requirements. He leads T57's platform, architecture, security and integrations, and its technology team.

Zaid AdiCo-founder and CPO

Zaid is a digital product expert with more than 8 years in UX and UI, turning complex processes into intuitive, scalable platforms, including for government and food-security projects. He leads T57's product and customer journey.

Muneeb HanbaliCo-founder and CCOLinkedIn

Muneeb has more than 15 years across infrastructure and food-linked sectors, leading large multi-stakeholder project teams from strategy to execution. He leads corporate communication and relations with stakeholders and partners, and is a member of T57's Saudi Board.

Dr. Mohamad Basheer KarubiCo-founder and Head of Corporate GovernanceLinkedIn

Dr. Karubi has more than 20 years in corporate governance, government relations and strategic advisory, including OPEC advisory and stakeholder engagement across the US, MENA, Africa and Asia. He leads governance and compliance.

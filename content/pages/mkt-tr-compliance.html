Know the rules for food trade in Türkiye before you ship.

Check requirements for your productSee how it works

T57's regulatory guide summarizes the main bodies, documents and rules for food trade in Türkiye, and shows on each trade what is required.

The challenge

The challenge

Food shipments are held at borders every day because of a missing certificate, a wrong label or an unregistered product. Rules differ by country and by product, and they change.

Complex requirements

Different documents for each product and destination.

Changing rules

Requirements are updated often.

Costly mistakes

Errors mean delays, rejections and spoiled goods.

Key bodies and rules in Türkiye

Ministry of Agriculture and ForestryFood safety, import controls and export health and phytosanitary certificates.
Ministry of TradeCustoms procedures, through the BILGE customs system, and export rules.
Halal Accreditation Agency (HAK)Accreditation of halal certification bodies.
Revenue Administration (GİB)Electronic invoicing (e-Fatura and e-Arşiv) for eligible businesses.
Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK)Governs personal data.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this guide legal advice?

No. It is a summary to help you prepare. Always confirm requirements with the relevant authority or your customs broker.

How often is the guide updated?

T57 reviews it regularly.

Can T57 handle customs clearance for me?

T57 connects you with licensed customs brokers, who handle clearance on the shared trade record.

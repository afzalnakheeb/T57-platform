The problem

Finding reliable logistics takes too long

For most food SMEs, booking logistics means calling several forwarders, waiting for quotes in different formats and hoping the chosen provider can handle perishable goods. Prices vary widely, capacity disappears in peak season, and route disruptions, like the 2026 closure of the Strait of Hormuz, force last-minute changes. Choosing the wrong partner can mean spoiled cargo, missed delivery windows and lost buyers.

Slow, opaque quoting

Quotes arrive late, in different formats and with hidden charges.

Unreliable partners

It is hard to know which providers can be trusted with chilled, frozen or fragile food.

Disruption

Route closures and capacity shortages leave shippers scrambling.

FAQ

Common questions

How are logistics partners vetted?

Partners go through business verification, license and insurance checks, and their performance is rated on recorded shipments.

Can I invite my own forwarder?

Yes. Your existing forwarder can join the trade record and quote alongside network partners.

Does T57 negotiate freight rates?

T57 aggregates demand and shows comparable quotes, which helps members get competitive rates. Members choose and book.

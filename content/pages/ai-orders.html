TodayWith T57

T57's ordering AI solves the gap between how food is actually ordered, by message and voice, and the structured records trade needs, using language models trained on food trade terms.

The problem without AI

Why this is hard without AI

Restaurants, retailers, wholesalers and farmers order by WhatsApp, phone and voice note, in many languages and with local product names. Orders are misread, quantities are disputed, and nothing flows into invoices, stock or finance. Asking these businesses to fill in web forms usually fails, because it does not fit how they work.

How the AI works

Data in, a small local model, a person decides

Model typeA language model fine-tuned for food trade, with speech-to-text for voice notes. Regional models handle Arabic dialects, Hindi, Malay, Turkish and local product names.
Data inputsThe message or voice note. The member's catalog, past orders and usual suppliers. Product names, units and pack sizes used in the region.
OutputA structured order (product, quantity, unit, price, supplier, delivery date and address), shown to the member as a short summary to confirm or correct. Unclear items are flagged with a question, never guessed silently.

Use case · Illustrative example

Riyadh grocery chain store manager reorders by voice

Before

Store managers at a Riyadh grocery chain sent orders to suppliers by WhatsApp. Misread quantities caused stock-outs and disputes, and head office reconciled invoices by hand.

With T57 AI

A manager sends a voice note in Arabic: "Same as last Sunday, plus five more cases of laban." T57 builds the order from last Sunday's, adds the laban, and asks one question about pack size. The manager confirms, and the supplier receives a structured order linked to the chain's records.

The challenge

The real risk is not production. It is the buyer.

Margins in food sales and exports are built, or broken, in procurement, documentation, payments and the quality of buyer relationships. T57 brings these parts together into a single, transparent environment. Sell at home too: list your products for local supermarkets, restaurants and offices.

Unreliable buyers

The real risk is not knowing if the buyer is genuine or likely to honor terms once the cargo is on the way. A single failed transaction can wipe out a season's profit.

Export paperwork is scattered across email, messaging apps and spreadsheets. One missing or inconsistent document can mean delays, demurrage or rejection.

Use case · Illustrative example

Indian rice exporter scales faster with trusted trade infrastructure

A mid-sized Golden Sella and Basmati rice exporter in India traditionally sold to a handful of known buyers in the Middle East, relying on brokers for both leads and collections. The exporter joined T57 as a club member and completed the KYB and capacity verification process.

Using T57's price and market intelligence, the exporter identified strong demand–supply signals from Malaysia and East Africa and responded to RFQs from two new buyers, negotiating via T57's AI-assisted tools and locking each order into a smart contract with escrow and independent quality inspection. All documentation was generated and stored in a single order workspace, with automatic sharing with financiers and insurers.

Within one season, the exporter expanded from one to three active markets, shortened its average payment cycle by more than two weeks, and reduced document-related disputes to near zero thanks to end-to-end traceability.

FAQ

Questions sellers ask

How does T57's virtual trade desk help in disputes and risk management?

The virtual trade desk acts as a risk manager. In the event of disputes, the desk steps in to mediate, enforce agreed-upon terms and protect the seller's interests. If a buyer becomes insolvent or defaults, T57 shifts to a commission-based recovery and resale approach, helping redirect goods or recover value.

How does a platform help with the financing gap?

Dedicated trade platforms that provide rich transaction and compliance data can lower perceived risk for financiers, improving access to credit and reducing the cost of capital. T57 is designed to expose this data in an auditable way so that funders can underwrite your deals with greater confidence.

Why is documentation and certification such a big issue in food exports?

Food exports are subject to stringent import-country inspection and certification regimes. Incomplete or incorrect certification can lead to rejection or non-compliance at the border. A system that centralizes documents, links them to lots and shipments, and validates certifications reduces the risk of costly rejections and delays.

Can better intelligence really help in volatile export markets?

Agricultural prices remain exposed to extreme weather, changing biofuel policies and rising trade barriers. Exporters using structured market, crop and price intelligence are better able to adjust sourcing and pricing to protect margins.

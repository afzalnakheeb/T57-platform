Built to be used by everyone.

Our accessibility standard, what we have built in, the known gaps and how to tell us about a problem.

T57 wants t57.ai to be usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or voice control, and people who prefer less motion.

Our standard

We design and test the site against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We believe the site is partially conformant: most pages meet the standard, and the known gaps are listed below.

What we have built in

  • A skip link, a logical heading order and one main heading on every page.
  • Breadcrumbs, a section bar and previous and next links on every page, so you always know where you are.
  • Full keyboard access to the menu, search, forms and interactive charts, with a visible focus outline.
  • Text alternatives for images, and labels on charts, maps and explainer animations, which carry no sound.
  • Animations, page transitions and video stop when your device is set to reduce motion, and every explainer has a pause button.
  • Colour contrast that meets AA for text, and layouts that reflow on small screens and when zoomed, without sideways scrolling.

Known limitations

  • Some animated charts announce their final values only, not each step of the animation.
  • Older downloadable documents may not be fully tagged for screen readers. Ask us and we will send an accessible version.
  • The site is in English only for now. Arabic, Malay and Turkish versions are in preparation.

How we test

We test every release with automated checks (axe-core and Lighthouse) across all pages, and check key journeys by keyboard and with a screen reader. This statement was last reviewed on 28 October 2026.

Tell us about a problem

If something on the site does not work for you, write to hello@t57.ai with the subject "Accessibility", or call +966 56 971 22 22. Tell us the page and what happened. We reply within five business days and can provide the information in another format.

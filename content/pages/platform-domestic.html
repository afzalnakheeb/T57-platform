T57 has two trade engines. One connects countries. The other works inside each country: it lets a local factory, farm or cooperative sell direct to the small and mid-sized supermarkets, restaurants, hotels, cafés and offices around it.

The problem

A good local product still struggles to reach the shelf

A new brand can get the taste, packaging and price right and still not reach buyers. Large retailers ask for listing fees, rebates, high margins, paid promotions and strict return terms before a product goes on the shelf. Smaller shops and restaurants buy through layers of distributors, so they pay more and the maker earns less. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Costly shelf access

Listing fees, rebates and promotions lock out new and small brands.

Too many middlemen

Each layer adds cost, so buyers pay more and makers keep less.

Cash and credit

Small buyers need time to pay, and small makers cannot wait for it.

How T57 works for you

List → Order → Deliver → Pay

  1. 01

    List

    A verified factory, farm or cooperative lists its products at factory price, with a minimum order such as 2, 5 or 20 cartons and lower prices for larger orders.

  2. 02

    Order

    Supermarkets, minimarkets, restaurants, hotels, cafés and offices find the products and order what they need, in the app or by chat.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    A local logistics partner is booked from the order, collects from the factory and delivers to the door, with tracking on the same record.

  4. 04

    Pay

    The buyer pays into escrow, or uses pay-later terms from a licensed B2B credit partner. The maker is paid when the delivery is confirmed.

FAQ

Common questions

Does T57 charge listing fees or rebates?

No. There are no listing fees, slotting rebates or forced promotions. Sellers set their own prices. T57 membership options are on the Pricing page.

Who delivers the orders?

Licensed local logistics partners, booked from the order. Sellers can also invite the delivery company they already use.

Can buyers pay later?

Yes, where a licensed B2B credit or pay-later partner approves the buyer. T57 does not lend.

What is the minimum order?

Each seller sets it, for example 2, 5 or 20 cartons, with lower prices for larger orders.

Can farms and cooperatives sell this way?

Yes. Farms, cooperatives and food processors can list fresh and packed products for local business buyers.

Which countries is this for?

Domestic B2B trade is built for each of T57's founding markets: Saudi Arabia, the UAE, India, Malaysia and Türkiye.

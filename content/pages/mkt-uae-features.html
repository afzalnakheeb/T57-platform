Every T57 capability is available to members in the UAE, with regulated and physical services delivered by licensed local partners.

The challenge

The challenge

Trading food means dealing with buyers, documents, banks, insurers, inspectors and shippers, often through separate systems. On T57, they all connect to one trade record.

Available in the UAE

Verified membershipKYC, KYB, site visits and the T57 Passport, by T57's local team.
Trade executionRequests for quote, quotes, contracts and invoices, for domestic and cross-border trade.
Trade financeInvoice financing, buyer credit and letters of credit from licensed UAE banks and finance companies.
Escrow and paymentsThrough licensed partners, in AED and any currency the partner supports.
InsuranceCargo and trade credit cover from licensed insurers.
Logistics and inspectionFreight, forwarding and inspection partners, booked from the trade record.
Documents and complianceDestination requirements and document checks, connected to UAE customs systems where available.
AI intelligencePricing, forecasting, supply status and matching, with models for Gulf import and re-export flows.
LanguageArabic and English.

FAQ

Common questions

Are all features available in every market?

Yes. The partners that deliver regulated services differ by country.

Can I pay in AED?

Yes, through licensed payment and escrow partners.

Who provides finance in the UAE?

Licensed UAE banks and finance companies. T57 does not lend.

FAQ

Common questions

Why do manufacturers and enterprises need a digital trade workflow now?

Digital trade workflow is important for countering high losses, volatility, and operational friction in the industry. FAO reports 13.3% food loss before retail, while the World Bank points to ongoing food inflation and uneven commodity movement across markets.

How does T57 help reduce procurement risk?

T57 helps structure RFQs, centralize bid visibility, and support trade in a more verified counterparty environment. In volatile markets, faster sourcing decisions and improved documentation can help protect supply continuity and margins.

Why is settlement speed so important in food and agri trade?

Delayed settlement can slow shipments, tie up working capital, and undermine confidence in the trade relationship. Digital platforms that accelerate settlement improve market access and business efficiency.

Can T57 support governance, audit readiness, and compliance?

Yes. T57 is designed to connect documents, milestones, counterparties, and payment records within a single, trackable workflow. This makes it easier for enterprise teams to support internal controls, reviews, and audit requirements across complex trade operations.

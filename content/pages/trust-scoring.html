Finance, escrow and insurance come from licensed partners.

T57's trust rating solves the problem of judging new partners on reputation alone, by building a rating from verified checks and real trade behavior on the platform.

The problem

In food trade, reputation travels by word of mouth.

Good small producers struggle to prove they are reliable, and buyers and lenders fall back on size and familiarity. Reviews on open platforms are easy to fake. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

No proof of reliability

Good performers have no way to show their record.

Fake reviews

Ratings that anyone can write are easy to game.

Opaque scores

Scores that cannot be explained cannot be trusted or improved.

How the trust rating works

Verified start → Trade behavior → Counterparty feedback → Explained results

  1. 01

    Verified start

    Each member's rating begins with their verification level and role.

  2. 02

    Trade behavior

    Completed trades, on-time delivery, document accuracy and payment performance, all taken from the trade record, build the rating.

  3. 03

    Counterparty feedback

    Only parties to a completed trade can rate each other.

  4. 04

    Explained results

    Members see what drives their rating and how to improve it.

FAQ

Common questions

What affects my trust rating?

Your verification, completed trades, on-time delivery, document accuracy, payment performance and feedback from your trading partners.

Can I see my own rating?

Yes, with the main factors behind it.

Can I dispute a rating?

Yes. You can contest any rating or feedback, and a person reviews it.

Do new members start at zero?

New members start with a rating based on their verification, which grows as they trade.

Our story

One question, asked in more than 60 countries

T57 began with one question: why does food that exists not reach the people and markets that need it? Through AlMukarramah, T57's founding team designed the strategy and ecosystem of the Islamic Organization for Food Security (IOFS), the OIC's food security institution. Over four years, the team travelled to more than 60 countries and met ministries, buyers, sellers, processors, cooperatives and farmers. Producers, traders and governments were rarely short of food or of willing partners. They were short of three things:

Access to markets

Producers and small traders cannot reach or prove themselves to buyers beyond their own networks.

Access to technology

Trade still runs on phone calls, messages and paper, with no shared record of a deal.

Access to finance

Working capital rarely reaches the smaller businesses that need it most.

The story behind our name

T57 is a promise written as a name: T, the values every trade must stand on, 5 industries brought together on one record, and 7 outcomes we work to deliver for every member.

T

Trade, built on trust, transparency and traceability

Trade is what we enable. Trust, transparency and traceability are how. Trust: every member is verified before they trade. Transparency: every party sees the same terms, documents and status on one record, and every AI recommendation comes with its reasons. Traceability: every trade can be followed from source to buyer, with each document and step recorded and tamper-evident.

5

Five industries, one trade

A single food trade needs five industries working together. T57 connects them on one record:

  • Agriculture and food productionWhat is grown, raised, caught, processed and made.
  • TradeBuyers and sellers, at home and across borders.
  • LogisticsFreight, cold chain, warehousing, inspection and customs.
  • FinancePayments, escrow, letters of credit, trade finance and insurance.
  • TechnologyVerification, trade records, blockchain and AI.

7

Seven outcomes, from local trade to global food security

  1. 1Food security

    Earlier warning of shortages and more sources of supply for the communities and countries that depend on imported food.

  2. 2Less food lost

    Surplus matched to buyers before it spoils, so more of what is grown reaches people.

  3. 3Integration

    One connected food system, where producers, buyers, banks, logistics providers and governments work on the same record, and trade moves from quote to settlement in days, not weeks.

  4. 4Access to finance

    Verified, documented trades that banks can finance, helping close the trade finance gap for smaller businesses.

  5. 5Higher incomes

    Fairer prices and direct access to buyers, so more of the value of food stays with those who produce it.

  6. 6Inclusion

    Smallholders, cooperatives, women-owned businesses and SMEs brought into formal trade, finance and markets.

  7. 7Resilience

    Food systems that adapt to climate, conflict and supply shocks, with alternative origins and routes ready when they are needed.

Trade built on trust, transparency and traceability, across five industries, delivering seven outcomes. That is T57.

FAQ

Common questions

Where is T57 based?

T57's operating company is in Dubai, its regional headquarters is in Riyadh, and it has teams in India and Malaysia, with country teams in all five founding markets.

What does the name T57 mean?

T stands for trade, built on trust, transparency and traceability. The 5 stands for the five industries T57 connects: agriculture and food production, trade, logistics, finance and technology. The 7 stands for the seven outcomes we work toward: food security, less food lost, integration, access to finance, higher incomes, inclusion and resilience.

Who owns T57?

T57 is founder-led.

TodayWith T57

T57 connects verified food producers, traders and buyers in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, India, Malaysia and Türkiye, on one trade record that works within each country and across their borders. These are the first five. T57 is open online to verified businesses in 195 countries and opens new markets phase by phase.

The challenge

The world spent an estimated $2.22 trillion on food imports in 2025 (FAO).

Much of that trade flows between the regions T57 serves: Gulf economies that import most of their food, and producing nations in South Asia, Southeast Asia and Türkiye that want to sell more of it, directly. Yet each market has its own rules, documents, languages and ways of doing business, and trust across borders is hard to build.

Fragmented markets

Each country has different rules, documents and platforms.

Trust across borders

Buyers and sellers in different countries cannot easily verify each other.

Local needs

Global platforms rarely fit local languages, crops, regulations and payment habits.

Trade both ways, between every market

Every founding market is both a buyer and a seller on T57. A member in any one of them can buy from, or sell to, verified members in all the others, on the same trade record. For example:

Saudi Arabia ↔ IndiaRice, spices, fresh produce and meat to the Kingdom; Saudi dates and processed food to India.
Saudi Arabia ↔ MalaysiaHalal processed food and palm products to the Kingdom; Saudi dates to Malaysia and ASEAN.
Saudi Arabia ↔ TürkiyeFruit, nuts, vegetables and pasta to the Kingdom; Saudi dates and dairy to Türkiye.
UAE ↔ all marketsImports from India, Malaysia and Türkiye for the UAE and for re-export, and UAE re-exports onward.
India ↔ Malaysia ↔ TürkiyeSpices, rice, palm products, pulses, nuts and processed food moving between producing markets.

FAQ

Common questions

Which countries does T57 operate in?

Saudi Arabia, the UAE, India, Malaysia and Türkiye. More markets will follow.

Can I trade with a country that is not a T57 market?

Members in founding markets can buy from or sell to verified members in other founding markets.

Does T57 support domestic trade, or only exports?

Both. Domestic B2B trade and cross-border trade run on the same platform.

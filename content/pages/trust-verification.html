T57's verification process solves the problem of fake, misrepresented and unknown counterparties, by checking every member's identity, ownership, licenses and role before they can trade.

The problem

On open marketplaces, anyone can list a product.

A reseller can pose as a farm, a shell company can pose as an exporter, and a sanctioned party can hide behind a new name. Checking a new partner yourself takes weeks of calls, documents and site visits that most businesses cannot afford. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Misrepresented roles

Buyers cannot tell a producer from a middleman.

Fake or unlicensed businesses

Forged documents and expired licenses are hard to spot.

Hidden risk

Sanctioned or high-risk parties are difficult to screen without specialist tools.

How verification works

Apply → KYC and KYB → Physical verification → Role label and Passport

  1. 01

    Apply

    The business submits company documents, owners, licenses, bank details and the role it claims.

  2. 02

    KYC and KYB

    T57 checks owners and directors, company registration, beneficial ownership, sanctions and watchlists, and document authenticity. AI triages each file, and an analyst reviews it.

  3. 03

    Physical verification

    T57's country team visits farms, factories and warehouses, and checks proof of ownership, such as a land title, lease or production license, for anyone who claims to produce.

  4. 04

    Role label and Passport

    The member is labeled owner-producer, processor, trader or intermediary, and receives a verified T57 Passport.

Verification does not stop there. Expiry reminders, periodic re-verification and continuous screening keep every Passport current.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does verification take?

It depends on the business and whether a site visit is needed. T57 tells you what is outstanding at each stage.

Why does T57 visit my farm or factory?

To confirm that businesses claiming to produce really do. This is what lets buyers trust the owner-producer label.

Do I need to verify again for each trade?

No. Your T57 Passport is reusable. It is renewed periodically, and you update it when your details change.

What if my application is declined?

You receive a stated reason and can ask for a review by a person.

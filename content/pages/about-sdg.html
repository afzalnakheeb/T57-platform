Verified trade, measurable progress.

Turn verified trade into measurable progress on the Sustainable Development Goals.

Partner with us on impact

T57 contributes to the Sustainable Development Goals by bringing small producers and businesses into verified trade and finance, and by recording that progress on a trusted record.

The problem

The world is off track to end hunger by 2030 (SOFI 2026).

Development programs struggle to reach small producers at scale and to prove their impact. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Reach

Programs find it hard to reach smallholders and small businesses directly.

Proof

Impact claims rest on surveys rather than verified records.

Sustainability

Support ends when funding ends, unless producers gain lasting market access.

How T57 contributes

Onboard → Trade → Record → Report

  1. 01

    Onboard

    Cooperatives, smallholders and SMEs join with assisted onboarding.

  2. 02

    Trade

    They sell to verified buyers and access finance through partners.

  3. 03

    Record

    Each trade is recorded, so income, volumes and finance are measurable.

  4. 04

    Report

    Development partners see agreed, aggregated impact data.

Use case · Illustrative example

A development partner funds cooperative onboarding in India

A development partner funds T57 memberships and onboarding for farmer producer organizations in India. Within a season, the partner can see, from aggregated trade records, how many FPOs are trading, the value of their sales and the finance they have received.

FAQ

Common questions

Can T57 report impact for our program?

Yes. Partners agree the indicators, and T57 reports aggregated, permissioned data.

Does T57 claim to deliver the SDGs on its own?

No. T57 contributes to specific targets alongside governments, development partners and the private sector.

TodayWith T57

T57's crop planning AI solves the gap between what farmers grow and what buyers need, by recommending crops, timing and volumes from demand signals, local conditions and farm data.

The problem without AI

Farmers often plant what they planted last year or what earned the most last season.

When many do the same, prices collapse at harvest, while buyers struggle to find other crops they need. Soil, water and climate limits are not always considered, and demand signals from buyers rarely reach the farm. The result is gluts, shortages and wasted land, water and inputs.

How the AI works

Data in, a small local model, a person decides

Model typeRecommendation and optimization models built on small, local crop models, demand forecasts and farm constraints.
Data inputsSoil, water and climate data for the farm or region. Crop and yield models. Demand and price forecasts. RFQs and contract farming demand on T57. Input costs. The farmer's land, equipment, labor and preferences. Farmer knowledge recorded with consent.
OutputA recommended crop plan (crops, varieties, planting windows and areas), expected yields and margins, and analytics that track the season against the plan.

Use case · Illustrative example

Malaysian vegetable cooperative plans for supermarket demand

Before

Members of a vegetable cooperative in the Cameron Highlands all planted the same crops, and prices crashed when harvests came in together.

With T57 AI

On T57, the cooperative sees forecast demand from verified supermarket and HoReCa buyers in Kuala Lumpur and Singapore. The AI recommends a staggered planting plan across crops and members that fits each farm's land and water. Harvests are spread out, prices stay steadier, and the cooperative signs contract farming agreements for part of the volume.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the AI tell me exactly what to plant?

It recommends options with expected yields and margins. You decide, and you can adjust the plan at any time.

Can cooperatives plan for all their members?

Yes. Cooperatives can build plans across members' farms to balance supply and meet buyer demand.

Joining T57 in Türkiye takes four steps, with help from our local team at each one.

Four steps to your first trade

  1. 01

    Apply

    Register your business online and tell us your role: producer, processor, trader or buyer.

  2. 02

    Get verified

    Upload your documents. Our Türkiye team completes KYC and KYB checks and, for producers, a site visit.

  3. 03

    Set up

    Receive your T57 Passport, add your team, products and buying needs, and choose your membership.

  4. 04

    Trade

    Send or answer your first request for quote, and agree terms on one record.

What you will need

  • Trade registry record and tax number
  • Owner and director IDs
  • Membership of the relevant exporters' association for exporters
  • Food business registration or approval where relevant
  • Halal certificates if exporting halal products
  • Bank details

The exact list depends on your business and role. Our local team confirms it with you.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to visit an office?

No. T57 is a global SaaS platform, so you apply, get verified and trade online. To meet the team, email hello@t57.ai.

Who supports my country?

In the five pilot markets, the local team. Anywhere else, our global team, by chat or at hello@t57.ai.

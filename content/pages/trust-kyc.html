The problem

Why trust is hard in food trade

Businesses are run by people, and fraud usually starts with a false identity: a fake director, a stolen ID, or an unauthorized person signing a contract. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

False identities

Stolen or forged identity documents.

Unauthorized signers

People acting for a company without authority.

Sanctioned individuals

People who should not be trading at all.

How KYC works

Identify → Confirm → Screen → Authorize

  1. 01

    Identify

    Owners, directors and users submit identity documents.

  2. 02

    Confirm

    T57 checks document authenticity and confirms that the person matches the document.

  3. 03

    Screen

    Each person is screened against sanctions and watchlists.

  4. 04

    Authorize

    Each user is linked to the company with a defined role and signing authority.

FAQ

Common questions

Who needs to complete KYC?

Owners, directors and anyone who will act for the company on T57.

Is my ID shared with other members?

No. Other members see that you are verified, not your documents.

What if I leave the company?

The company removes your access, and your past actions stay in the audit trail.

Can I use one identity for several companies?

Yes. Each company link is verified separately, with its own role and authority.

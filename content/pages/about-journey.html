FAQ

Common questions

Who has T57 signed agreements with?

T57 and its founding company, AlMukarramah, have agreements with the Islamic Organization for Food Security (IOFS), the Government of the Chechen Republic, the Development Bank of Maldives, Food Contract Corporation of Kazakhstan, the International Assembly of Islamic Business, Dar Al Halal Nigeria and the Aquaculture Centre of Excellence in Singapore, and signed 150 MoUs and letters of intent at Gulfood 2026.

When did T57 start?

T57 was introduced at the Food Security Conference in Cape Town in August 2023 and pre-launched at the 2nd IOFS Food Security Forum in Doha in October 2023. It grew out of Trade57, built by AlMukarramah.

Are the MoUs binding?

Most are memoranda of understanding and letters of intent: frameworks for working together. Any subscription or commercial commitment is made in a separate agreement.

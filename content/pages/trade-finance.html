The problem

Weeks of separate applications

The global trade finance gap reached $2.5 trillion in 2025, and banks rejected 41% of SME trade finance applications (ADB, January 2026). Food SMEs are hit hardest. Each bank asks for the same documents in a different format, takes weeks to respond and often says no because it cannot see a clean record of the trade. So producers and traders fund deals from their own cash, turn down orders or accept expensive informal credit.

How it works

Request → Compare → Fund and repay

  1. 01

    Request

    From an accepted trade, send a finance request built from your verified deal file: Passport, contract, counterparties and documents.

  2. 02

    Compare

    Licensed banks and finance companies, including Islamic finance providers, review the same file and compete with offers.

  3. 03

    Fund and repay

    Accept the best offer. At settlement, the end buyer's payment goes into escrow and repays the lender automatically.

Why lenders trust T57

Bank blockchains became digital silos. T57 shows the whole trade.

Between 2022 and 2023, several bank-backed trade networks closed, including we.trade, Marco Polo, TradeLens and Contour. Each connected its own members and little else, so no one could see the whole trade. T57 starts from the trade, not the bank.

Every party in viewA lender reviewing a $10 million deal sees the buyer, the seller, the insurer, the freight forwarder, the shipping line and the inspector, all verified and on one record.
Pay as the trade movesSmart contracts release funds at each verified milestone: loading, departure, arrival and inspection. If something goes wrong at loading, payments stop, so exposure is the first tranche, not the whole deal.
One button, every lenderTap "I need trade finance" while you buy. Partner banks, fintechs, finance companies and crowdfunding platforms are alerted at once. Both sides are already KYC and KYB checked, with banks taking part in those checks, so approval is faster and lenders compete on price.

Use case · Illustrative example

Riyadh trader funds an Indian rice shipment

A Riyadh trading house has a confirmed order from a Saudi distributor for basmati rice from an Indian processor. Its own capital is tied up in other shipments. It sends one finance request from the trade record to several licensed lenders and accepts the best offer. The exporter is paid at shipment through an LC. When the distributor accepts delivery and pays into escrow, the lender is repaid automatically and the balance goes to the trader.

Why T57

We believe that a sound food trade should be financeable, whatever the size of the business behind it.

T57 does not lend

Finance is provided by licensed banks and finance companies, who make their own credit decisions. T57 provides the verified record and the workflow.

AI advises, people decide

AI helps prepare and check the deal file. Lenders decide, and any adverse decision by T57 about a member is reviewed by a person.

FAQ

Common questions

Does T57 provide the finance?

No. T57 does not lend or hold client funds. It sends your verified deal file to licensed lenders, who decide whether to fund the trade and on what terms.

What kinds of finance can I request?

Pre-shipment and post-shipment finance, receivables finance and letters of credit, through participating banks and finance companies, including Shariah-compliant options.

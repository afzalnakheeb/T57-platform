T57 solves slow, fragmented trade payments by giving members wallets in any currency and digital banking services from licensed partners, connected to every trade.

The problem

Money moves slower than the goods

A food business that trades across borders may hold accounts in several banks and currencies, wait days for payments to clear and lose money on every conversion. Payments sit in one system, contracts in another and invoices in a third, so finance teams spend hours matching them. For smaller exporters, opening a foreign-currency account can take weeks, and some are refused altogether.

How it works

Open a wallet → Pay and get paid → Reconcile automatically

Use case · Illustrative example

Chennai seafood exporter gets paid faster by a Dubai buyer

A Chennai frozen seafood exporter sells to a verified Dubai distributor every month. Both hold multi-currency wallets through T57's digital banking partner. When the distributor confirms delivery, escrow releases payment straight into the exporter's wallet, and it settles in near real time. The exporter converts what it needs to rupees, keeps dirhams for its next freight payment, and every transaction is already matched to its invoice.

Why T57

We believe that getting paid for food should be as fast as the food needs to move.

Licensed partners

Wallets, accounts and digital banking services are provided by licensed banks and payment institutions. T57 does not hold client funds.

One record for money and goods

Payments sit on the same trade record as contracts and deliveries, which makes finance, audit and compliance simpler.

FAQ

Common questions

Is T57 a bank?

No. Digital banking services on T57 are provided by licensed partners. T57 connects them to your verified profile and trade record.

Which currencies can I hold?

Any currency your trade needs. T57's banking partners hold, convert and settle across currencies, so you can pay suppliers and get paid by buyers in their own currency.

T57 is built to comply with the data protection laws of each of its founding markets, so members and partners can share data with confidence.

The problem

Cross-border trade means data crosses borders too.

Each founding market has its own data protection law, with its own rules on consent, storage and transfer. Businesses need a platform that respects all of them. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Different laws in every market

Rules on consent, residency and transfers vary.

Cross-border transfers

Data must move between markets lawfully.

Individual rights

People have rights to access, correct and delete their data.

How T57 protects data

Lawful basis → Local rules → Controlled transfers → Rights respected

  1. 01

    Lawful basis

    T57 collects only the data needed for verification, trading and compliance, with consent where required.

  2. 02

    Local rules

    Data handling follows each market's law, with data kept in-region where required.

  3. 03

    Controlled transfers

    Data moves across borders only as the law allows and as the trade requires.

  4. 04

    Rights respected

    Members can access, correct and request deletion of their data, subject to legal retention.

Use case · Illustrative example

A Malaysian cooperative shares farmer data safely

A cooperative in Kelantan onboards its member farmers to T57. Each farmer gives consent for their data to be used for verification and for anonymized crop forecasting. Their personal data stays protected under Malaysia's PDPA, and buyers in the Gulf see only the cooperative's verified Passport and product details.

FAQ

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On Microsoft Azure, in multiple regions. Where local law requires it, data is stored in-region.

How do I access or correct my data?

Through your account settings, or by contacting our data protection team.

Does T57 share my data with partners?

Only with your consent, or where a trade or the law requires it, such as sharing documents with a partner bank you chose.

Who is responsible for data protection at T57?

T57's data protection lead, who can be reached at hello@t57.ai.

Disconnected actors, limited financing, and scarce world-class expertise make it difficult to achieve optimal outcomes in the food and agri industry. T57 brings the entire chain together on a single intelligent platform, turning the efforts of farmers and producers, sellers and exporters, cooperatives and FPOs, buyers and importers, traders and aggregators, food processors, manufacturers and enterprises, and governments and regulators into real commercial outcomes.

The challenge

Disconnected actors and scarce expertise hold the system back

T57 is built as a collaboration engine for the real economy of food. Here, capital, deep tech, logistics capacity, and on-the-ground expertise compound each other's strengths. Whether you need the expertise of an agronomist, a cold-chain operator, or a university to access the next generation of agri talent, T57 is your platform for connecting and growing.

Fragmentation and information asymmetry across the value chain

Ecosystem participants operate in silos, with limited visibility into and access to agronomy and compliance experts, cross-border trade advisers, and others.

Compliance complexity and a lack of trusted expertise

Critical trade aspects, such as non-tariff measures, food safety standards, organic and halal certifications, and phytosanitary requirements, create a maze that raises costs and excludes producers from premium markets.

Limited pathways to monetize expertise and create measurable impact

Many experts, technology providers, universities, financiers, and service organizations have strong capabilities but lack access to real commercial opportunities.

FAQ

Common questions

How do T57 partners help me reach better markets?

T57's vetted partners help you meet standards, reliably move produce, and access premium capabilities to help you grow, without having to build these relationships yourself.

What kind of partners can I access on T57?

You can connect with a diverse range of partners who help you meet complex compliance requirements, reduce logistics losses, unlock financing and insurance, accelerate innovation, and turn more of your effort into predictable, higher-value trade outcomes.

How does T57 ensure that collaborators are trustworthy?

T57 uses a club-style model that includes due diligence, track-record checks, and ongoing performance data, ensuring that ecosystem participants work only with verified partners.

Why should governments and regulators care about the T57 partner network?

By providing access to trusted partners across the value chain, T57 helps users align their goals with the policy and regulatory boundaries established by governments.

T57's regulatory guide summarizes the main bodies, documents and rules for food trade in the UAE, and shows on each trade what is required.

The challenge

The challenge

Food shipments are held at borders every day because of a missing certificate, a wrong label or an unregistered product. Rules differ by country and by product, and they change. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Complex requirements

Different documents for each product and destination.

Changing rules

Requirements are updated often.

Costly mistakes

Errors mean delays, rejections and spoiled goods.

Key bodies and rules in the UAE

Ministry of Climate Change and Environment (MOCCAE)Federal rules on food imports, including plant and animal health.
Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology (MoIAT)Standards and the national halal system.
Local food authoritiesFor example, Dubai Municipality and the Abu Dhabi Agriculture and Food Safety Authority (ADAFSA), for food registration and inspection in each emirate.
CustomsEach emirate's customs authority, under the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security.
Personal Data Protection LawFederal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this guide legal advice?

No. It is a summary to help you prepare. Always confirm requirements with the relevant authority or your customs broker.

How often is the guide updated?

T57 reviews it regularly.

Can T57 handle customs clearance for me?

T57 connects you with licensed customs brokers, who handle clearance on the shared trade record.

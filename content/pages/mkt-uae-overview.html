The challenge

The challenge

The UAE is one of the world's great trading hubs: its non-oil foreign trade reached about AED 3.8 trillion ($1.03 trillion) in 2025, up about 27% on the year before (WAM). It imports most of its food, and its traders supply markets across the Gulf, Africa and Asia. Speed and trust matter, because food is perishable and margins are thin. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Supplier risk

Importers need verified suppliers and alternative origins at short notice.

Working capital

Traders tie up cash in goods in transit.

Document load

Re-exports multiply the documents and checks each shipment needs.

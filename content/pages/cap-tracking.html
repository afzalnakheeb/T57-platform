The problem

Blind spots between shipment and delivery

Once food leaves the warehouse, most businesses lose sight of it. Updates come by email from forwarders, carrier websites show different information, and temperature problems are discovered only when a container is opened. Buyers cannot plan, sellers cannot reassure them, and lenders and insurers have no independent evidence of what happened. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

No single view

Tracking information is spread across carriers, forwarders and ports.

Late discovery of problems

Delays and temperature breaches are found too late to act.

Weak evidence

Without recorded data, claims and disputes are hard to settle.

Use case · Illustrative example

Chittagong importer spots a reefer fault before it spoils a shipment

A Chittagong importer is bringing frozen chicken from a verified Brazilian processor. During transshipment, T57 alerts the importer and the processor that the container's temperature is rising. The forwarder is notified on the same record and has the reefer unit repaired at the port within hours. The cargo arrives in good condition, and the temperature log shows the buyer and insurer exactly what happened.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need special tracking devices?

No. T57 uses carrier and port data for all shipments. IoT devices add live temperature and location for sensitive cargo.

Who can see my shipment?

Only the parties to the trade and the partners you authorize, such as your lender or insurer.

Can tracking trigger payment?

Yes. Agreed milestones, such as arrival or delivery confirmation, can release escrow payments automatically.

T57's roadmap shows how the platform builds layer by layer, so members, partners and governments can see where it is going and plan with it.

The problem

Infrastructure people depend on cannot be built all at once

Food trade infrastructure cannot be built all at once. Trust has to come first, because finance and data are only valuable when they rest on verified members and records. Many platforms promise everything on day one and deliver little. T57 is built in deliberate stages, with each layer strengthening the next. The key challenges this approach addresses include:

Trust before scale

Verification and the trade record must be solid before finance and data can rely on them.

Partners at their own pace

Banks, insurers and authorities join and integrate on their own timelines.

Market by market

Each market opens when both its supply and demand sides are ready.

How the platform grows

Verified trade → Connected finance → Food ecosystem → Expanding markets

  1. 01

    Verified trade

    Membership verification, the T57 Passport and role labels, RFQs and quotes, contracts from quotes, matching, compliance screening, price data, and partner escrow, letters of credit, insurance and logistics.

  2. 02

    Connected finance

    Multi-lender finance requests, blockchain records and smart-contract milestones, forecasting, wallets in any currency, and deeper insurance and takaful.

  3. 03

    Food ecosystem

    Small, local AI models at scale, digital banking and alternative payments, trade data services, paperless pre-clearance with customs authorities, the food security view for governments, contract farming and the agronomy network.

  4. 04

    Expanding markets

    Starting with Saudi Arabia, the UAE, India, Malaysia and Türkiye, then opening further corridors across the OIC, ASEAN, Africa and beyond.

What each stage brings

4 ways each stage helps

For buyers and sellers

Verified partners and one trade record first, then easier finance, then deeper intelligence and services.

For lenders and insurers

Verified deal files first, then structured multi-lender requests and self-liquidating trades, then richer risk data.

For governments

Verified supplier data first, then pre-clearance and the food security view.

For farmers and cooperatives

Market access first, then contract farming, crop forecasting and agronomy support.

Use case · Illustrative example

Turkish cooperative grows with the platform

A hazelnut cooperative in Giresun joins T57 to reach verified buyers in the Gulf. It first uses verification, RFQs and escrow. As connected finance arrives, it uses multi-lender requests to fund the harvest. Later, it adopts crop forecasting and contract farming, so it can agree volumes with buyers before the season starts.

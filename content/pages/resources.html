T57 Resources brings together insight, research, news, videos, events and plain-language guides on digital food trade, food security, trade finance and AI.

The problem

Food businesses make big decisions with little time and scattered information.

Market reports are expensive, rules change often, and new technology such as AI and digital trade documents is hard to judge. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Scattered information

Prices, rules and market news spread across many sources.

Jargon

Trade finance, compliance and technology terms that put smaller businesses off.

Little time

Owners and managers need short, practical answers.

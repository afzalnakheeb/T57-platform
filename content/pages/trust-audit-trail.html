The problem

Why trust is hard in food trade

When a shipment is rejected, a payment is late or a regulator asks questions, businesses often cannot say who approved what, or which version of a document was used. Emails, chat messages and spreadsheets leave gaps, and gaps lead to disputes, fines and lost finance. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Missing history

Decisions and changes are scattered across inboxes and phones.

Version confusion

Nobody can prove which contract or certificate was final.

Hard audits

Auditors, lenders and regulators need evidence that takes weeks to collect.

How the audit trail works

Capture → Attribute → Protect → Share

  1. 01

    Capture

    Every action on a trade, such as a quote, edit, approval, signature, document upload or milestone, is recorded automatically.

  2. 02

    Attribute

    Each entry is linked to a verified person, their company and their role, with a timestamp.

  3. 03

    Protect

    Corrections are added as new entries, never written over old ones, and fingerprints of key records are anchored on a blockchain ledger, so any change can be detected.

  4. 04

    Share

    Members export the history for an auditor, lender or regulator, with consent.

FAQ

Common questions

Can anyone edit the audit trail?

No. Entries are tamper-evident: any change can be detected, and nothing is deleted. Corrections are added as new entries.

Who can see my trade's audit trail?

The parties to the trade, and anyone they choose to share it with, such as a lender or auditor.

How long are records kept?

For the period required by law in each market.

Are AI decisions recorded?

Yes. AI recommendations and the human decisions made on them are logged.

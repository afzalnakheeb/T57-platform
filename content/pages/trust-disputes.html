T57's dispute process solves the problem of costly, drawn-out trade disputes, by resolving them from the shared trade record, with escrow paused until there is an outcome.

The problem

Why trust is hard in food trade

When a cross-border food trade goes wrong, the goods are often perishable, the parties are in different countries, and the evidence is scattered. Court action is slow and expensive, so small businesses often just take the loss. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

No shared evidence

Each side holds different documents and messages.

Money at risk

Payments are made or lost before the facts are clear.

Slow, costly remedies

Cross-border legal action is out of reach for most.

How dispute resolution works

Raise → Pause → Review → Resolve

  1. 01

    Raise

    Either party opens a dispute on the trade record, stating the issue, such as quality, quantity, delay or documents.

  2. 02

    Pause

    Where the trade uses escrow, the licensed escrow partner holds the disputed amount, and partial release is possible for any undisputed part.

  3. 03

    Review

    Both sides add evidence, such as inspection reports, photos and logs, to the record. T57 helps the parties reach an agreement based on the agreed terms.

  4. 04

    Resolve

    The parties settle, or the dispute moves to the route set in their contract. The outcome is recorded, and escrow is released accordingly.

Use case · Illustrative example

A Riyadh importer and a Gujarat exporter settle a quality claim

A Saudi importer reports that part of a groundnut shipment is below the agreed grade. The disputed amount stays in escrow while the rest is released to the exporter. A partner inspector tests samples and confirms that 8% of the bags are below grade. The parties agree a price adjustment on the record, and escrow releases the balance.

FAQ

Common questions

What disputes does T57 cover?

Disputes over trades made on T57, such as quality, quantity, delivery, documents and payment.

Does T57 decide who is right?

T57 helps the parties reach an agreement based on the record and their contract. Where they cannot agree, the dispute follows the route set in the contract.

What happens to my money during a dispute?

If the trade uses escrow, the disputed amount is held by the licensed escrow partner until the dispute is resolved.

Does a dispute affect my trust rating?

The outcome is recorded. A fair resolution does not count against either side, and you can contest any rating effect.

The problem

A single food trade touches a dozen companies that do not share records

The global trade finance gap reached $2.5 trillion in 2025, and banks rejected 41% of SME trade finance applications (ADB, January 2026). One reason is that no one sees the whole trade. The buyer, seller, bank, insurer, forwarder, shipping line, inspector and customs authority each keep their own copy, in their own format. Trust is rebuilt at every step, documents are retyped, and perishable food waits. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Services bought one by one

Shipping, storage, insurance, inspection and finance are each found, priced and booked separately.

Lenders cannot see the trade

Banks see paperwork, not the parties, goods and milestones behind it, so they say no or price high.

Clearance starts too late

Documents often reach the port only when the goods do, and food sits waiting.

What makes it different

Built for the depth and scale of food trade

From one pallet to a national reserveThe same platform runs a single container or a tender for hundreds of thousands, even millions, of tonnes of wheat or rice for a national food reserve. For governments, this means one verified record for their largest purchases.
Three ways to set a priceBuyers request quotes. Governments and large buyers run tenders. Sellers list a lot, such as premium Madinah dates, and let verified buyers bid. For sellers, this means the best market price.
Know your rice down to the paddyA listing can carry hundreds of details. For rice, that covers paddy variety, field pesticides, soil, milling, chemicals and polish. For buyers, this means specifying exactly what they want.
Lenders see the whole tradeBank blockchains became digital silos, and several bank-backed trade networks closed in 2022 and 2023, including we.trade, Marco Polo, TradeLens and Contour. On T57, a lender sees every party to the trade: buyer, seller, insurer, forwarder, shipping line and inspector. For banks, this means lower risk.
Finance that follows proofSmart contracts release a lender's funds at each verified milestone. If something goes wrong at loading, payments stop, so the lender's exposure is the first tranche, not the whole deal.
One button, many lendersA finance request alerts partner banks, fintechs, finance companies and crowdfunding platforms at once. Both sides are already KYC and KYB checked, so approval is faster and lenders compete on price.
Your forwarder, or a rated oneInvite the freight forwarder you already use, or choose a vetted one in your city by rating and reviews. For shippers, this means no change of partner is forced.
Cleared before arrivalWhere a port runs pre-arrival clearance, its authority can review the shipment's checked documents before the ship docks. For importers, this means food reaches the warehouse faster.
Everyone is ratedBuyers, sellers, forwarders, experts, logistics partners and lenders are all rated after each trade. Repeated upheld complaints lead to removal.
Phase two: carbon income for farmersThrough specialist partners, farmers will be able to earn from carbon their land captures, measured, verified and sold to companies that must offset emissions. This service is planned for phase two.

Use case · Illustrative example

A Jeddah importer brings in 5,000 tonnes of rice

A Jeddah importer posts an RFQ for 5,000 tonnes of parboiled rice, specifying the paddy variety, milling grade and maximum pesticide residue. Three verified Indian mills quote, and the importer accepts the best one; the contract is generated from the quote. The mill invites its usual freight forwarder, books sea freight and IoT trackers, and the importer adds cargo takaful. The importer taps "I need trade finance": four partner lenders compete, and the winner releases funds at loading, departure and arrival. Documents reach Jeddah before the ship does. On arrival the container is sampled, the lab approves the rice, and it goes on sale. Every party is then rated.

FAQ

Common questions

Does T57 lend money or move the goods?

No. T57 is the platform. Licensed banks and finance partners fund trades, licensed partners provide escrow and insurance, and logistics partners move and store the goods.

Can I keep my current freight forwarder?

Yes. Invite them to T57 and they work on the same trade record, or choose a vetted forwarder in your city by rating and reviews.

How do smart contracts protect a lender?

Funds are released in stages as milestones such as loading, departure and arrival are verified. If a milestone fails, later payments stop.

Which ports support pre-arrival clearance?

T57 prepares and checks documents before shipment for every trade. Where a port or customs authority runs pre-arrival clearance, the documents can be reviewed before the goods arrive.

Is there a size limit on a trade?

No. T57 is built for anything from a single container to national food-reserve tenders of millions of tonnes.

When will carbon credits be available?

Carbon income for farmers is planned for phase two, through specialist partners.

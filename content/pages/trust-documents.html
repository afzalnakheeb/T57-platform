T57's document management system solves the problem of missing, wrong and forged trade documents, by creating, checking, storing and sharing them on the trade record.

The problem

Why trust is hard in food trade

A single food shipment can need a commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, phytosanitary or health certificate, halal certificate, inspection report and bill of lading. One wrong field or expired certificate can hold cargo at the border, where perishable food loses value every day. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Errors and omissions

Documents that do not match each other or the destination's rules.

Forged or expired documents

Certificates that are fake or out of date.

Scattered files

Documents spread across email, couriers and shared drives.

How document management works

Create → Collect → Check → Share and verify

  1. 01

    Create

    T57 generates documents such as invoices and packing lists from the agreed contract, so data is entered once.

  2. 02

    Collect

    Partners such as inspectors, insurers and forwarders upload their documents straight to the trade record.

  3. 03

    Check

    AI checks documents against each other and against the destination's requirements, and flags gaps for a person to review.

  4. 04

    Share and verify

    Authorized parties access documents securely, and anyone authorized can check a document's fingerprint against the ledger.

A Bursa cherry exporter clears its first shipment to Riyadh

FAQ

Common questions

Which documents can T57 generate?

Commercial documents such as invoices, packing lists and contract-based forms. Official certificates are issued by the relevant authority or partner and uploaded to the record.

Does T57 check my documents?

AI checks for gaps and mismatches and flags them. Your team and your partners make the final decisions.

Who can see my documents?

Only the parties you authorize for each trade.

Are original paper documents still needed?

In some markets and for some documents, yes. T57 shows where paper originals are still required.

The problem

Why pricing matters

A cooperative making its first export and a trading house running hundreds of shipments a year need different things. Paying the same for both would be unfair to one of them. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Starting small

New and small businesses need a low-cost way in.

Growing volume

Active traders need lower costs per trade as they grow.

Team needs

Larger businesses need more users, AI capacity, controls and support.

FAQ

Common questions

Which plan should I choose?

If you trade occasionally, start with Explore. As a rough guide, the trade fee savings on a higher plan pay for its higher membership once your monthly trade volume is large enough. The ROI calculator shows the break-even for your business.

What does the free trial include?

You can build your profile, explore verified members and market intelligence, use AI tools and send requests for quote. Verification, contracts, escrow and finance start when you subscribe, because verification involves real checks by our team and partners. Founding members get 90 days free at launch.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. You can move up at any time, and move down at renewal.

T57 solves the slow, scattered way food is quoted today, for buyers who need the right supply and sellers who want serious enquiries.

The problem

Quotes in different formats, from suppliers you cannot check

Most food trade still starts with an enquiry sent by email, WhatsApp or a broker. Quotes come back in different formats, currencies and Incoterms, often from suppliers the buyer cannot check. Specifications get lost in long message threads, and the terms finally agreed are retyped into a contract by hand. Buyers waste days comparing offers that are not like for like. Sellers waste time answering enquiries that were never serious.

How it works

Request → Bid → Award

  1. 01

    Request

    A verified buyer sets product, grade, quantity, packaging, Incoterms, destination and delivery window once.

  2. 02

    Bid

    Verified suppliers matched to the request submit quotes in the same structure, so price and terms line up.

  3. 03

    Award

    The buyer compares bids side by side, accepts one, and the contract is generated from the accepted quote.

What makes it different

Built for trust, not just listings

Only verified biddersEvery supplier holds a T57 Passport and a role label (owner-producer, processor, trader or intermediary). Buyers know who they are dealing with before they read a price.
Like-for-like comparisonStructured quotes and recent price data sit side by side. Buyers can see which offer is strongest and how it compares with the market.
From quote to contract with no retypingThe accepted quote becomes the contract, with the same product, price, quantity and terms. Both parties work from one trade record from the first message.
Or let buyers bidSellers can list a lot, such as premium Madinah dates, set a reserve price and a closing time, and let verified buyers bid. The best price wins, with escrow and the contract already in place.

Use case · Illustrative example

Dubai importer sources frozen poultry from Türkiye

A Dubai food importer needs 10 containers of halal frozen poultry for a hotel group contract. It posts one RFQ on T57. Within days, five verified processors in Türkiye and Brazil submit structured bids. The importer filters for owner-producers and processors, compares price, payment terms and delivery dates side by side, and awards the contract to a processor in Türkiye. The contract, inspection booking and LC request all start from the same record.

FAQ

Common questions

Who can respond to an RFQ on T57?

Only verified members whose products, capacity and markets match the request. Buyers can also invite specific suppliers.

Can sellers see each other's bids?

No. Each bid is visible only to the buyer, which keeps pricing fair and confidential.

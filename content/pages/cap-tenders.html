The problem

Qualified suppliers locked out of public buying

Governments buy food at scale for strategic reserves, schools, hospitals, the military and food aid. Since the 2026 closure of the Strait of Hormuz, food security and reliable supply have moved even higher up the public agenda. Yet tenders are often published as long documents in different formats and languages, with strict eligibility and document rules. Smaller and overseas suppliers struggle to find them or answer them correctly, while procurement teams receive bids they cannot easily verify or compare.

Hard to find

Tenders are scattered across portals, gazettes and agencies, and many qualified suppliers never see them.

Hard to answer

Eligibility rules, specifications and document requirements are complex, and small errors disqualify good bids.

Hard to verify

Procurement teams need confidence that bidders are real, capable and compliant.

Use case · Illustrative example

Malaysian processor joins a consortium to supply a school meals tender

A small frozen food processor in Selangor sees a state school meals tender on T57 but cannot meet the volume alone. The AI summary shows the eligibility rules and required certificates, and the eligibility check flags one missing food safety document. The processor uploads it, forms a consortium with a verified poultry producer and a cold-chain logistics partner, and submits through the authority's portal. After winning, every delivery is inspected and recorded for the authority's audit.

FAQ

Common questions

Does T57 submit bids on my behalf?

No. T57 helps you find, understand and prepare a response. You submit it through the issuing authority's official channel.

Can government buyers use T57 to run tenders?

Government buyers can invite verified T57 members to bid and track delivery on T57, alongside their own procurement rules and systems.

Which countries' tenders are covered?

Tenders from T57's founding markets first (Saudi Arabia, the UAE, India, Malaysia and Türkiye), with more markets added over time.

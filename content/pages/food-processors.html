T57 addresses the structural friction that food processors face due to unreliable raw material supply, margin pressure from price volatility, working capital constraints, slow payments, and weak documentation.

The challenge

Unreliable inputs leave capacity idle

With T57, food processors bridge the gap between volatile agricultural supply and consistent, safe food products, operating capital-intensive plants and mills where disruptions in sourcing, financing, or settlement affect capacity utilization and margins. T57 is built for this reality, helping processors secure a dependable raw material supply, protect margins against input price swings, and manage working capital and trade execution through a single connected operating platform. Significant value loss before raw materials reach the plant

For processors, leakage and delays in upstream supply translate into unreliable, variable quality, and a higher risk of underutilized capacity. Better-structured sourcing and faster trade execution help convert raw materials into reliable, processable input.

Input price volatility and planning for food inflation

Food prices often move differently from general inflation, so the timing of procurement, contract structure, and hedging becomes critical while plants must keep running.

Complex trade and documentation flows strain already-lean teams

Food processing is tightly regulated and documentation-intensive, with multiple functions generating records for every batch and shipment.

Use case · Illustrative example

Edible-oil processor streamlines cross-border sourcing

A regional edible oil processor sources crude palm oil from multiple suppliers for its private-label products across Asia. Price volatility has been high, and scattered RFQs and manual documentation are creating delays and reconciliation issues.

With T57, the processor runs RFQs for each variety, inviting only vetted suppliers with visible capacity and performance history. Bids arrive on a single dashboard, making it easier to compare prices, terms, and reliability and to lock in deals in time for production planning. High-value contracts are supported by escrow and rapid eLC workflows, improving supplier confidence and enabling earlier shipment. As shipments move, all documents and payment events are captured in a single audit trail, providing a shared view of each contract across the enterprise.

FAQ

Common questions

Why is digitizing trade especially important for food processors?

Processors depend on timely and predictable raw material supplies and operate on tight margins. Supply assurance and trade execution platforms make inputs more predictable, optimize capacity utilization, and protect margins in volatile markets.

Does T57 help with working capital and financing challenges?

Partner banks, escrow structures, faster eLC workflows, and tokenized-trade capabilities are designed to shorten settlement cycles and connect real trade to innovative finance solutions. For processors, that can mean less cash stuck in transit and more capacity to invest in inventory, maintenance, or growth.

How does T57 help reduce supply and loss-related risks?

T57 supports more reliable sourcing by structuring RFQs, working with vetted counterparties, and creating better visibility across deals.

Can T57 support compliance and audit requirements for processing plants?

Yes. T57 keeps trade, payment, and documentation data linked within a single audit trail, supporting internal controls and external audits. This helps processors meet the documentation demands of regulators and financial compliance frameworks.

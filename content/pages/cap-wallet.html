TodayWith T57

Finance, escrow and insurance come from licensed partners.

T57 solves the scattered, slow way food businesses pay and get paid, by giving every verified member a wallet in any currency from licensed partners, connected to the trade record.

The problem

Payments scattered across channels

Food businesses pay many parties on every trade: suppliers, freight forwarders, inspectors, insurers and customs brokers. They receive payments from buyers in different currencies and through different banks. Each payment needs a bank transfer, a reference and a manual match to an invoice. Fees and exchange spreads add up, and finance teams spend hours reconciling. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Too many payment channels

Different banks, apps and references for each counterparty and service provider.

Slow settlement

Cross-border payments take days and lose value to fees and spreads.

Manual reconciliation

Payments are not linked to the contracts and invoices they relate to.

How T57 works for you

Open your wallet → Fund it → Pay → Reconcile

  1. 01

    Open your wallet

    Verified members open a wallet with T57's licensed payment partner, using the checks already done for their T57 Passport.

  2. 02

  3. 03

  4. 04

    Reconcile

    Every payment is matched automatically to its contract, invoice and trade record.

Use case · Illustrative example

Bengaluru coffee exporter pays partners and gets paid in one place

A specialty coffee exporter in Bengaluru sells to verified roasters in Dubai and Istanbul. On T57 it pays its inspection partner and freight forwarder in rupees from its wallet, and receives the Dubai buyer's payment in dirhams and the Istanbul buyer's in euros. Each payment is already matched to its invoice, and the exporter converts only what it needs, at a rate shown upfront.

FAQ

Common questions

Is the T57 wallet a bank account?

It is an e-money or payment account provided by a licensed partner. For full banking services, see Digital Banking.

Can I pay people who are not on T57?

Yes. You can pay external suppliers and service providers by bank transfer from your wallet, subject to the partner's checks.

What are the fees?

Fees and exchange rates are set by the payment partner and shown before you confirm each payment.

TodayWith T57

Finance, escrow and insurance come from licensed partners.

T57 partners with banks, insurers, logistics and inspection providers, technology companies and institutions, so every verified trade on T57 can be financed, insured, moved and checked by trusted specialists.

The problem

T57 does not lend, hold client funds, own goods or provide transport.

It connects the specialists who do. For partners, the challenge is reaching good customers in food trade at a cost that works. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Costly customer acquisition

Finding and checking smaller food businesses one by one is expensive.

Poor data

Banks and insurers lack reliable records to price risk.

Disconnected workflows

Partners receive documents by email and re-key them.

FAQ

Common questions

Does T57 compete with partners?

No. T57 does not lend, insure, ship or inspect. It brings partners verified customers and better data.

How do partners get paid?

Members pay partners for their services directly. T57 may earn a small commission from some partners, as agreed in each partnership.

Can partners in any country join?

Partners licensed in T57's markets, or serving trade with them, can apply.

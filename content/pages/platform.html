The problem

Food trade has no shared identity, record or intelligence

Food is one of the largest traded goods in the world. The global food import bill reached a record of about $2.22 trillion in 2025 (FAO, June 2026). Yet the trade still runs on disconnected systems: each bank, insurer, forwarder, inspector and customs authority keeps its own records, in its own format, about the same trade. Nothing connects them, so trust is rebuilt at every step, and errors and fraud slip through the gaps. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

No shared identity

Every party verifies every other party from scratch.

No shared record

The same trade exists in dozens of disconnected systems and documents.

No shared intelligence

Data that could improve pricing, finance and food security stays locked in silos.

How the platform works

Trust layer → Trade layer → Services layer → Intelligence and record layers

  1. 01

    Trust layer

    Verifies every member (KYC/KYB, physical checks and compliance screening) and issues the T57 Passport and role labels.

  2. 02

    Trade layer

    Runs RFQs, quotes, contracts, orders and the Trade Desk on one trade record.

  3. 03

    Services layer

    Connects licensed partners for payments, escrow, letters of credit, finance, insurance, logistics, inspection and customs through APIs.

  4. 04

    Intelligence and record layers

    AI models support every step, and key records are anchored on a tamper-evident blockchain ledger.

Platform components

8 parts, one platform

Identity and verification

KYC, KYB, physical verification and the T57 Passport. For every member, this means verified once and trusted everywhere on T57.

Trade record

A single, shared record of every quote, contract, document, milestone and payment. For all parties, this means one version of the truth.

Trade workflow

RFQs, quotes, contracts from quotes, orders and contract farming. For buyers and sellers, this means structured, consistent deals.

Partner integration hub

API connections to banks, payment institutions, insurers, takaful operators, logistics firms, inspectors and customs systems. For members, this means services inside the trade.

Intelligence layer

Small, local AI models for matching, pricing, forecasting, risk, compliance and documents. For members, this means expert support at every step.

Blockchain layer

Tamper-evident anchoring of key records, tokenized trade documents and smart-contract milestones. For lenders and regulators, this means records they can trust.

Security and data protection

Multi-region cloud infrastructure on Microsoft Azure, designed for data residency, encryption and access control. For members, this means data that is protected and stays where the law requires.

Open APIs

Integration with members' ERP, accounting and procurement systems. For larger businesses, this means T57 fits into their existing tools.

Use case · Illustrative example

Riyadh food group connects its systems to T57

A Riyadh food group runs its own ERP for purchasing and finance. It connects the ERP to T57 through APIs, so RFQs, contracts and invoices flow both ways. Its suppliers are verified on T57, its bank receives verified deal files for finance, and its customs broker receives documents before shipments arrive. The group's teams keep using their own systems, while every trade runs on one shared, trusted record.

FAQ

Common questions

Is T57 a marketplace or a platform?

T57 is a platform: a members-only verified trade club with the infrastructure to run trades end to end. Members trade directly with each other on it.

Can T57 connect to my existing systems?

Yes. T57 offers APIs for ERP, accounting and procurement systems, and connects to partner banks, insurers and logistics providers.

Where is my data stored?

On Microsoft Azure cloud infrastructure across regions, designed to meet each market's data-residency requirements.

The problem

Quotes you cannot compare

Food sourcing still runs on emails, calls and messaging apps. A buyer describes what it needs differently to each supplier, and quotes come back in different units, currencies and Incoterms. Comparing them takes days, and the agreed terms are then retyped into a contract and purchase order, often with errors. Sellers, meanwhile, spend hours answering enquiries from buyers they cannot check. The key challenges T57 addresses include:

Quotes you cannot compare

Different formats, units and terms make it hard to see which offer is truly best.

Unverified parties

Buyers and sellers often cannot confirm who is on the other side, or whether they are the producer or an intermediary.

Errors between quote and contract

Retyping terms into contracts and orders creates mismatches that surface at the bank, the port or the warehouse.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does an RFQ stay open?

The buyer sets the deadline. Bids received after it closes are not shown unless the buyer reopens the RFQ.

Can I negotiate after receiving bids?

Yes. You can ask clarifying questions or request a revised bid from any supplier before you award the contract.

Can sellers create offers without an RFQ?

Yes. Verified sellers can publish product listings and wholesale terms that buyers can respond to directly.

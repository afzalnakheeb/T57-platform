Plan break-even

When a higher plan pays for itself

Your numbers, your assumptionsThe calculator does not use promised savings. It shows the result of the inputs you choose.
Fees includedMembership and trade fees are built in, so the result is net.
Plan break-evenBecause higher plans cost more a month and less per trade, the calculator shows when upgrading pays. On standard prices and on cost alone, Growth costs less than Explore once you trade more than about $157,000 a month, and Enterprise costs less than Growth above about $375,000 a month.

Use case · Illustrative example

From the calculator

An Istanbul dried fruit exporter trades about $200,000 a month on Explore. The calculator shows that Growth would cost it less in total, because the lower trade fee saves more than the higher membership, and it would gain forecasting and priority support too. It upgrades to Growth.

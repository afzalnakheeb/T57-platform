The problem

One person doing sourcing, pricing and compliance

Large trading houses and banks run trade desks: teams that find counterparties, check prices, draft contracts and screen every party for sanctions. Most food SMEs have none of this. One person handles sourcing, pricing, paperwork and compliance across email, spreadsheets and PDFs. Deals are slower, mistakes are common, and compliance gaps can stop a shipment at the border or a payment at the bank.

How it works

Match → Price and agree → Check

  1. 01

    Match

    The Trade Desk ranks verified counterparties by product, volume, corridor and track record.

  2. 02

    Price and agree

    Recent price data supports your quote or counter-offer, and the accepted quote becomes the contract.

  3. 03

    Check

    Every counterparty is screened against sanctions and watchlists, and documents are checked for completeness before the deal moves on.

Use case · Illustrative example

Kochi spice exporter wins its first Gulf buyer

A family spice exporter in Kochi has always sold through agents. On T57, the Trade Desk suggests three verified distributors in Riyadh and Jeddah that buy cardamom and pepper in matching volumes. Price data shows where the exporter's quote sits against recent trades. When a Jeddah distributor accepts, the contract is generated from the quote, both parties are screened automatically, and a missing phytosanitary certificate is flagged before shipment, not at the port.

FAQ

Common questions

Is the Virtual Trade Desk a person or software?

Both. The Trade Desk is software that matches, prices, drafts and screens, supported by T57's trade desk team for cases that need human review.

Does the Trade Desk negotiate for me?

No. It gives you matches, price data and checks. You negotiate and decide.

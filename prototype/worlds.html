    It is hard to tell producers from intermediaries, or real companies from shell companies.

    Disconnected services

    Finance, insurance, inspection and logistics are bought separately, with no shared view of the trade.

    Uneven access

    Smaller producers and SMEs struggle to reach buyers, lenders and fair terms that larger players take for granted.

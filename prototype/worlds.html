    Who we serve · Governments and regulators

    Building real-time, governable food systems for public good

    T57 gives governments and regulators a real-time, policy-ready view of their agri and food ecosystem, linking intelligence, procurement, and capital so food security becomes a measurable, governable asset rather than a recurring crisis.

    Request a briefingSee how it works
    Motion graphic: food corridors light up between surplus and deficit regions

    The challenge

    Delayed data turns supply shocks into crises

    Food systems around the world are fragmented. Data is delayed, procurement is opaque, and policy often reacts to crises rather than anticipating them.

    Fragmented, slow food-security intelligence

    Ministries often rely on scattered reports, manual spreadsheets, and diplomatic emails to understand supply, demand, and risk, which makes early-warning and coordinated action difficult.

    Opaque, high-risk public procurement

    Large food tenders are vulnerable to leakage, fraud, payment delays, and quality failures, undermining public trust and budgets.

    Limited visibility to align policy, markets, and investment

    Policy decisions on trade, subsidies, storage, and investment often proceed without a clear, data-backed picture of surpluses, deficits, food waste, or SME capacity across regions and value chains.

    How T57 works for governments and regulators

    From live intelligence to clean procurement

    1. 01

      Strategic onboarding and integration

      A dashboard aligned with your food-security agenda, connected through APIs to your procurement, customs and audit systems.

    2. 02

      Live intelligence and early warning

      Live dashboards, AI early warning and "what-if" modeling of tariff, quota, subsidy and trade-route options.

    3. 03

      Clean, transparent procurement

      RFQs to vetted sellers, with smart contracts and escrow releasing funds only when milestones are verified.

    4. 04

      From insight to investment

      Banks, sovereign funds, impact capital and CSR programs connected to vetted food-security projects.

    Use case

    Stabilizing wheat supply during a regional shock

    The signal

    A mid-sized importing country faces early signs of disruption to its wheat supply as a key exporter announces potential export restrictions following a poor harvest. The AI-driven early-warning module has already flagged emerging reports of production shortfalls, giving the team valuable lead time.

    The response

    Officials open the surplus–deficit map to identify alternative origins, run scenarios in the policy sandbox, and publish an RFQ to vetted suppliers, linked to government-issued tender IDs and terms.

    The outcome

    Faster, cleaner procurement; moderated domestic price pressures; and a medium-term strategy to reduce vulnerability to similar shocks.

    Slow diligence and weak visibility hold capital back

    T57 eliminates slow due diligence and weak transaction visibility that constrain capital, and goes beyond listing opportunities to manage execution, disputes, documentation and follow-through.

    Opaque counterparties, incomplete diligence

    Run both sides of every deal on one record, and fund it faster

    Traders make food trade work. They find supply, bundle volumes, take on risk and move goods across borders. T57 brings your suppliers, buyers, finance and logistics onto one verified record.

    Animated infographic: quotes and orders meet in one deal file, and lenders compete to fund it

    The challenge

    Traders carry both sides of the risk, often on their own balance sheet

    Lenders cannot see a clean record of the trade. Banks rejected 41% of SME trade finance applications in 2025, and the global trade finance gap reached $2.5 trillion (ADB, January 2026).

    Tied-up capital

    Traders often pay suppliers before buyers pay them, which limits how many deals they can run.

    Two-sided risk

    A failure by the supplier or the buyer lands on the trader, and paperwork rarely lines up across both.

    Trust deficit

    Intermediaries are sometimes seen as adding cost without value, so honest traders need a way to prove theirs.

    How T57 works for you

    From verified profile to a funded, executed deal

    1. 01

      Get verified

      Receive your T57 Passport with a trader or intermediary role label and a visible track record.

    2. 02

      Source and sell

      Send RFQs to verified suppliers and answer RFQs from verified buyers, all from one workspace.

    3. 03

      Fund the trade

      Send one deal file to several licensed lenders who compete to finance it.

    4. 04

      Execute

      Book inspection, insurance or takaful, freight and customs through partners, and track both sides on one trade record.

    With T57

    The outcome

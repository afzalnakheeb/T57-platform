    Disconnected services

    Finance, insurance, inspection and logistics are bought separately, with no shared view of the trade.

    Uneven access

    Smaller producers and SMEs struggle to reach buyers, lenders and fair terms that larger players take for granted.

    Why should a farmer or producer use a digital trade platform like T57?

    T57 provides access to verified buyers and transparent payment processes. This reduces uncertainty and improves income realization.

    What should farmers watch out for in contract farming?

    Contract farming deals are often between stronger buyers and weaker farmers. This could result in unfair terms, over-dependence on a single buyer, or disputes over quality and deductions. Farmers can access T57's expertise to negotiate the best terms.

    Can better market access really reduce waste?

    Yes. Better matching of supply with demand, along with more coordinated trade execution, can reduce losses between harvest and market.

    Why is finance such a major issue for producers?

    The World Bank notes that agriculture finance remains disproportionately limited in many developing markets due to risk, transaction costs, and the difficulty of serving fragmented rural borrowers. That is why trade-linked and digitally enabled finance models matter for producers who need working capital tied to real orders.

    Does price intelligence really matter at the farm level?

    Yes. When producers lack visibility into demand and prices, they negotiate from a weaker position and often rely too heavily on intermediaries. T57's core model directly addresses this by improving access to market and price intelligence.
